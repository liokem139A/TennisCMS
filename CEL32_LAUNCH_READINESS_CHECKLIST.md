## CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS

1. **Club contact information must be 100% accurate**
   - Wrong email = failed delivery, wasted opportunity
   - Verification: Double-check spelling, test sends

2. **Email templates must be personalized**
   - Generic email won't get responses from busy club executives
   - Each email should reference club-specific details

3. **Dashboard must track real-time metrics**
   - Need visibility to opens/clicks by 24-hour mark
   - Enables fast decision-making on follow-ups

4. **Response SLAs must be met**
   - Tier 1 responses need 2-hour follow-up
   - Quick response time shows respect and moves deals forward

5. **Call scheduling must happen in real-time**
   - As soon as clubs show interest, lock in call time
   - Don't let momentum die waiting for next day's batch send

## RISKS & MITIGATIONS

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Wrong club contact emails | Campaign fails | Verify via website, LinkedIn, phone call |
| Email goes to spam | No opens/responses | Test email delivery, monitor spam folder |
| Calendly link broken | Can't schedule calls | Test link before sending, have phone number backup |
| Dashboard not tracking | Can't make decisions | Set up manual backup tracking in spreadsheet |
| No opens by 24 hours | Campaign at risk | Prepare escalation email or phone follow-up ready |
| Bounces/delivery issues | Reduced reach | Check bounce report at 1-hour post-send, get corrected emails |
