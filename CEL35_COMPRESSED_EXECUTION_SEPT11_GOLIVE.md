## RISK MITIGATION

### Risk 1: Fewer than 2-3 prospects willing to commit by Sept 11
**Mitigation**: 
- Target 5-8 "hot" prospects in discovery (not just top 3)
- Prioritize Local Club (fastest 1-2 week close) as guaranteed close
- Have Tier 3 fallback prospects (Google, Local Insurance) ready

### Risk 2: Negotiation delays on contract terms
**Mitigation**:
- Pre-approve standard terms with Finance/CEO by Sept 3
- Prepare 2-3 package variations (e.g., $50k vs $75k vs $100k)
- Have sponsor agreements templates ready (not drafted on the fly)

### Risk 3: Integration complexity delays close
**Mitigation**:
- Define MVP integration: Basic sponsorship + 2 activation touchpoints
- Detail plan can extend into Sept 12+
- Get commit on core package; details follow-up

### Risk 4: Key decision-maker unavailable
**Mitigation**:
- Identify backup contacts during discovery calls
- Schedule flexibility (virtual + phone options)
- Have 2-3 alternative time slots prepared

### Risk 5: Prospect needs Board approval (CVS/Aetna)
**Mitigation**:
- Identify internal process upfront during discovery
- Target Tier 1 local prospects that decide faster
- Build relationship with right stakeholder early
