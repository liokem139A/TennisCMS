# Ambassador Onboarding & Training Plan

**Purpose**: Comprehensive onboarding and training curriculum for all ambassador tiers  
**Timeline**: Started Sept 26, 2026; Ongoing throughout tenure  
**Audience**: All ambassadors, program coordinators, trainers

---

## Onboarding Philosophy

Our onboarding approach is **engaging, bite-sized, and action-oriented**. We prioritize:
- **Clarity**: Clear role expectations and responsibilities
- **Connection**: Building community among ambassadors
- **Competence**: Equipping ambassadors with tools and knowledge
- **Culture**: Instilling program values and brand standards
- **Confidence**: Supporting ambassadors to succeed

## Initial Training (3 Hours Total)

### Timing & Format

**Cohort-Based Training**
- Group of 10-15 ambassadors per cohort
- Multiple cohorts running Sept 26-30
- Mix of synchronous (live) and asynchronous (self-paced) components
- Recording available for asynchronous viewing

### Module 1: Welcome & Community Vision (15 minutes)

**Synchronous Session**
- Live video greeting from Program Director or CEO
- Program mission, vision, and values (animated video)
- Impact data from previous initiatives
- Q&A with program leadership

**Deliverables**
- Recorded welcome video
- Mission statement card (printed and digital)
- Program overview one-pager
- FAQ document

**Outcomes**
- [ ] Understand program mission and vision
- [ ] Feel welcomed and valued as ambassador
- [ ] Know who to contact with questions

---

### Module 2: Communication & Brand Voice (20 minutes)

**Synchronous Session**
- Live training on how to represent the program
- Brand guidelines walkthrough (visual identity, tone, messaging)
- Examples of good and poor communication
- Interactive scenarios and feedback

**Key Topics**
1. Program taglines and key messages
2. Tone of voice (authentic, inclusive, enthusiastic)
3. What to say and what NOT to say
4. Social media guidelines
5. Representing the program professionally
6. Inclusive and welcoming language

**Deliverables**
- 10-page brand guidelines document
- Communication templates (email, social posts, event descriptions)
- Example social media content (sharable)
- Quick reference card

**Outcomes**
- [ ] Understand program brand and messaging
- [ ] Know how to represent program professionally
- [ ] Have templates and examples to use

---

### Module 3: Activity Planning & Event Facilitation (30 minutes)

**Synchronous Session**
- Live walkthrough of activity planning process
- Case studies of successful club activities
- Interactive planning exercise
- Resource review and Q&A

**Key Topics**
1. Types of activities (socials, tournaments, clinics, casual play)
2. Step-by-step planning process
3. Budget and logistics
4. Promotion and attendance strategies
5. During-event facilitation tips
6. Post-event follow-up and feedback

**Deliverables**
- Activity Planning Playbook (50 pages)
  - 10 pre-planned activity templates (ready to adapt)
  - Email templates for promotion
  - Checklist for before, during, after
  - Budget templates
- Social media graphics (editable templates)
- Event evaluation survey
- Resources library (games, icebreakers, activities)

**Outcomes**
- [ ] Understand activity planning process
- [ ] Have ready-to-use templates and plans
- [ ] Confident to host first event

---

### Module 4: Ambassador Portal Demo & Tools (20 minutes)

**Synchronous Session**
- Live screen share walkthrough of portal
- Demonstration of key features
- Practice activities (enter data, submit report)
- Troubleshooting and tech support

**Key Features Covered**
1. Dashboard and performance metrics
2. Event calendar and RSVP management
3. Resource library and templates
4. Reporting system and monthly submissions
5. Communication tools (messaging, announcements)
6. Leaderboard and recognition system
7. Account settings and preferences

**Deliverables**
- Portal User Guide (20 pages with screenshots)
- Video tutorials (5-10 minutes per feature)
- Troubleshooting guide
- Tech support contact information

**Outcomes**
- [ ] Comfortable navigating portal
- [ ] Know where to find resources
- [ ] Know how to submit reports

---

### Module 5: Role-Specific Training (Choice of 20 minutes)

**Club Ambassador Track**
- Recruitment strategies (how to identify and approach prospects)
- Retention tactics (keeping members engaged)
- Monthly check-in template
- Success stories and tips from top ambassadors
- Practical exercise: draft recruitment message

**Regional Coordinator Track**
- Team management essentials
- Ambassador support and coaching
- Cross-club event planning
- Escalation and issue resolution
- Leadership competencies
- Practical exercise: create action plan for ambassadors

**Program Ambassador Track**
- Content creation and social media
- Public speaking tips and tricks
- Media training essentials
- Collaborating with marketing team
- Event appearance logistics
- Practical exercise: create social post or video content

**Deliverables** (per track)
- 15-20 page role-specific playbook
- Templates and checklists
- Case studies and examples
- Resource library specific to role

**Outcomes**
- [ ] Clear on specific role responsibilities
- [ ] Have tools and templates for role
- [ ] Know what success looks like

---

### Module 6: Live Q&A & Celebration (15 minutes)

**Synchronous Session**

- Celebration of cohort joining

**Content**

## Post-Training Support (Weeks 1-4)

### Week 1: Foundation Building

**Day 1-2 Post-Training**
- [ ] Send follow-up thank you email
- [ ] Assign first micro-task (e.g., complete profile, join Slack)
- [ ] Schedule individual 1-on-1 call
- [ ] Provide personalized onboarding buddy (peer ambassador)

**1-on-1 Call with Coordinator** (30 minutes)
- Clarify role and expectations
- Address any concerns or questions
- Set first month goals
- Discuss local context and opportunities
- Identify 3-5 key prospects for recruitment
- Confirm communication preferences

**Onboarding Buddy Program**
- Each new ambassador paired with experienced ambassador
- Buddy is same role or regional coordinator
- Weekly check-in (15-30 min) for 4 weeks
- Answer questions and provide encouragement
- Share tips and best practices

**First Micro-Tasks**
- [ ] Complete ambassador profile in portal
- [ ] Join private Slack channel
- [ ] Submit first recruitment target list (3-5 prospects)
- [ ] Confirm receipt of onboarding materials

**Resources Provided**
- Buddy contact information
- Welcome email from buddy
- First week action checklist
- Slack channel invitation

---

### Week 2: First Activity Planning

**Your First Event**
- Support coordinator helps plan first event
- Choose from pre-planned templates or custom plan
- Date: End of first month (late Oct)
- Goal: 15-25 attendees
- Format: Social event or casual tournament

**Week 2 Tasks**
- [ ] Meet with coordinator to finalize event plan
- [ ] Book venue and send invitations
- [ ] Promote event on social media and email
- [ ] Gather equipment or supplies needed
- [ ] Confirm attendance numbers

**Resources & Support**
- Pre-built event plans (5 options)
- Promotion email templates
- Social media graphics
- Planning checklist
- Weekly check-in with buddy

**Milestones**
- Event details posted in portal
- Invitations sent to prospects
- Minimum 10 confirmed attendees
- All logistics confirmed

---

### Week 3: Recruitment & Momentum

**Active Recruitment Launch**
- Begin outreach to identified prospects
- Schedule personal conversations
- Host small group information sessions
- Gather applications and referrals

**Week 3 Tasks**
- [ ] Contact 10-15 prospects via email or phone
- [ ] Have 5-10 personal conversations
- [ ] Host 1 small group info session (optional)
- [ ] Submit first monthly recruitment report
- [ ] Attend weekly group training call

**Resources & Support**
- Recruitment email templates
- Conversation talking points
- Information session slides
- Monthly report template
- Weekly group training call

**Milestones**
- 10-15 prospects contacted
- 5+ personal conversations completed
- 2-5 referrals generated
- First month report submitted

---

### Week 4: First Event Execution & Reflection

**Host First Event**
- Execute planned first event
- Facilitate inclusive, welcoming environment
- Capture attendance and feedback
- Follow up with attendees

**Week 4 Tasks**
- [ ] Host first organized event
- [ ] Capture photos/testimonials
- [ ] Collect attendance and feedback
- [ ] Send thank you to attendees
- [ ] Submit event report and metrics

**Resources & Support**
- Event day checklist
- Facilitation tips and techniques
- Photos/content capture guidelines
- Post-event survey template
- Event report template

**Milestones**
- Event executed successfully
- 15-25 attendees participated
- Post-event survey completed
- Follow-up thank yous sent

**Reflection & Celebration**
- Debrief call with coordinator
- Celebrate wins and learnings
- Identify improvements for next event
- Recognize contribution to community

## Ongoing Training & Development

### Monthly Training Calls (Ongoing)

**First Friday of Each Month, 10am-11am**
- Rotating topics based on ambassador feedback
- 30-40 min training, 20-30 min Q&A
- Small group breakouts by role
- Recorded for those who can't attend

**Sample Topics**
- Month 1: Retention and member engagement
- Month 2: Event budgeting and logistics
- Month 3: Conflict resolution and difficult conversations
- Month 4: Recruitment strategies and tips
- Month 5: Social media content and promotion
- Month 6: Self-care and burnout prevention
- Ongoing: Guest speakers, peer sharing, best practices

**Deliverables**
- Monthly agenda and slides
- Recorded training video
- Handouts and checklists
- Action items and homework

---

### Quarterly Regional Meetups (In-Person)

**Timing**: Mid-month (every 13 weeks)
- 3-4 hour in-person gathering
- 5-10 ambassadors per session
- Mix of training, networking, and celebration

**Format**
- Lunch and networking (30 min)
- Role-specific breakouts (60 min)
- Skills training session (60 min)
- Group activities and team building (30 min)
- Program updates and Q&A (30 min)

**Content**
- Peer learning and best practice sharing
- Advanced training on role-specific topics
- Recognition and celebration of top performers
- Product updates and roadmap
- Feedback collection for program improvement

---

### Certification Programs (Optional)

**Basic Certification** (Self-paced, 4-6 hours)
- Modules: Community leadership, event planning, recruiting, retention
- Assessments: Quizzes, practical exercises, reflection journals
- Certificate: Digital badge + printed certificate
- Timeline: 4-8 weeks
- Recommended: For all ambassadors

**Advanced Certification** (In-person workshop, 8 hours)
- Modules: Advanced event planning, team leadership, strategic planning
- Assessments: Group projects, presentations, peer feedback
- Certificate: Advanced digital badge + printed certificate
- Timeline: 12-16 weeks
- Recommended: For Regional Coordinators and top performers

**Specialized Certifications** (Role-specific)
- **Recruitment Specialist**: Advanced recruitment, outreach, conversion
- **Event Director**: Large event planning, sponsorships, logistics
- **Community Builder**: Retention, engagement, member experience
- **Marketing Ambassador**: Content, social media, influencer skills

## Common Questions During Onboarding

**Q: How many hours per month will I spend?**
A: Club Ambassadors typically spend 5-8 hours/month. Regional Coordinators spend 10-12 hours/month. Program Ambassadors spend 3-5 hours/month. Many ambassadors find it rewarding and don't notice the time passing!

**Q: Do I have to have tennis experience?**
A: No! We welcome all experience levels. Your passion for the community is what matters most.

**Q: What if I don't know many people at my club?**
A: That's okay! Part of being an ambassador is building relationships. We'll help you with recruitment strategies and talking points.

**Q: How much money will I earn?**
A: Club Ambassadors can earn $50-100/month in performance bonuses, plus additional quarterly and annual bonuses for top performers. Regional Coordinators earn $75-125/month. It's not meant to be a full-time income, but it's a nice bonus for your volunteer effort.

**Q: What if I need to take a break?**
A: Life happens! Let us know if you need a temporary pause. We have flexible arrangements for ambassadors with temporary conflicts.

**Q: How do I know if I'm doing a good job?**
A: You'll see real-time feedback in the portal dashboard, monthly check-ins with your coordinator, and recognition on the leaderboard. We also celebrate wins publicly in our newsletter and community calls.
