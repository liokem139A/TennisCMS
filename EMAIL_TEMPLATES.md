**CEL-32: Campaign Assets**

## Template 1: Initial Outreach (Tier 1 Priority)
**Subject Line A** (Testing): "Partnership Opportunity with [Company Name]"
**Subject Line B** (Testing): "How [Your Company] + [Partner Company] Can [Achieve Goal]"

```
Hi [First Name],

I've been following [Company Name]'s work in [industry/space], and I think there's a 
significant opportunity for us to collaborate on [specific area].

Here's why I reached out:
• [Pain point 1 they likely face]
• [Pain point 2 they likely face]  
• [Specific strength/capability you offer]

I'd love to have a quick 20-minute conversation to explore whether this makes sense for you. 
Are you free for a call this week? I can work around your schedule:
• [Specific day/time option 1]
• [Specific day/time option 2]
• [Specific day/time option 3]

If none of those work, just let me know your availability.

[Resource link]: Here's a brief overview of our approach → [Link to 1-pager/case study]

Looking forward to connecting,

[Your name]
[Your title]
[Your company]
[Contact info]
[Company website]
```

## Email Personalization Placeholders

### Mandatory Fields
- `[First Name]` - Individual's first name
- `[Company Name]` - Partner organization name
- `[Industry/Space]` - Their industry vertical
- `[Calendly link]` - Your personal Calendly or scheduling link

### Recommended Fields (Conditional)
- `[Specific pain point 1/2]` - Research from company data
- `[Specific capability]` - Tailor to their needs
- `[Specific section they clicked]` - From engagement tracking
- `[Case study reference]` - Relevant to their industry
- `[Resource link]` - 1-pager, white paper, etc.

## Subject Line Testing Strategy

### A/B Testing Groups (Split 50/50)
**Version A**: Direct value prop
- "Partnership Opportunity with [Company Name]"
- "How [Your Company] + [Partner] Can [Outcome]"

**Version B**: Curiosity/Specific angle
- "[First Name] - Quick partnership idea for [Company Name]"
- "Potentially Relevant: [Company Name] + [Your Company]"

### Metrics to Track
- Open rate (primary)
- Click rate (secondary)
- Response rate (tertiary)
- Time to open

### Decision Rule
If Version A ≥90% of Version B engagement: use A going forward
Otherwise: continue testing with new variations

## Compliance & Legal

### Email Footer
```
© [Year] [Your Company]. All rights reserved.
[Address] | [Phone]

Unsubscribe | Update Preferences | View in Browser
```

### Required Language
- Clear unsubscribe option in every email
- CAN-SPAM compliant address
- Honest subject line (no misleading claims)
- Response request respects GDPR/local regulations

### Contact Frequency
- Maximum 3 touches in 72-hour window
- Minimum 3-day gap between sequences for same person
- Remove from sequence immediately if "unsubscribe" received

## Best Practices Summary

1. **Personalization Depth**: Minimum 2 specific details about their company (not generic)
2. **Value First**: Lead with what's in it for them, not what you want
3. **Action Clarity**: Make the next step obvious (link, yes/no question, specific time)
4. **Length**: Keep under 150 words if cold, slightly more if responding
5. **Tone**: Conversational, human, no corporate jargon
6. **Mobile Friendly**: Test on mobile, keep above-the-fold content clear
7. **Proof**: Reference social proof or case studies when possible
8. **Respect**: Always offer an easy opt-out, acknowledge their time
