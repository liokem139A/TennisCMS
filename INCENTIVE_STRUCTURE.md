# Ambassador Program Incentive Structure

**Program**: Tennis Community Ambassador Program  
**Timeline**: September 6, 2026 - Ongoing  
**Target**: 50-100 Ambassadors  
**Budget**: $45,000 - $60,000 annually (75-ambassador baseline)  
**Owner**: Finance & Program Management

---

## Overview

The incentive structure combines monetary bonuses with non-monetary recognition to motivate and reward ambassador performance. The program is designed to:

- **Recognize** top performers at every performance level
- **Reward** consistent engagement and results
- **Retain** ambassadors long-term through meaningful benefits
- **Scale** sustainably within the annual budget ($14,600 for Phase 2 recruitment execution)

---

## Tiered Incentive Framework

### Tier 1: Monthly Incentives (All Ambassadors)

Every ambassador receives monthly recognition and benefits:

**Recognition & Access**
- Digital badge and community recognition (profile in ambassador portal)
- Early access to new community features
- Exclusive ambassador group chat and networking forums
- Monthly newsletter featuring ambassador spotlights

**Tangible Rewards**
- Monthly swag (branded items: hats, socks, water bottles, stickers)
- 15-20% discount on program merchandise (apparel, equipment)
- Monthly performance bonus pool allocation

**Performance Bonus Tiers (Monthly)**
- **Club Ambassador**: $50/month for 90%+ performance
- **Regional Coordinator**: $75/month for 90%+ performance  
- **Program Ambassador**: $100/month for content/engagement targets

---

### Tier 2: Quarterly Incentives (Top 25% Performers)

Recognized based on:
- Recruitment targets (5-10+ new members recruited)
- Event hosting consistency (1-2+ events per month)
- Community feedback ratings (4.5+ stars)
- Attendance at program events (80%+ participation)

**Quarterly Rewards**
- **Bonus**: $200-500 per quarter ($600-1,500 annually)
- **Premium Gear**: Branded jackets, bags, premium apparel
- **Exclusive Invitations**: VIP event access, networking opportunities
- **Marketing Spotlight**: Feature in program newsletter, social media, website
- **Sponsorship Connections**: Introduction to brand partners for collaboration

---

### Tier 3: Annual Incentives (Top 10% Performers)

Elite performers recognized for exceptional year-round contribution:

**Annual Rewards**
- **Bonus**: $1,000-2,000 annual bonus
- **Luxury Gifts**: Tech items, experience vouchers, premium gifts
- **"Ambassador of the Year" Award**: Public recognition, award ceremony
- **Premium Benefits**: Free access to coaching clinics, training programs
- **Leadership Opportunity**: Speaking slot at annual ambassador summit
- **VIP Access**: Direct line to product and community leadership team

---

## Non-Monetary Incentive Program

Recognizing that many ambassadors are motivated by community impact and professional development:

### Recognition
- **Monthly Leaderboards**: Top 10 ambassadors by recruitment, events, engagement
- **Community Spotlights**: Feature stories in newsletters and social media
- **Annual Awards**: "Most Improved," "Community Champion," "Best Event Host," etc.
- **Public Recognition**: Annual ambassador summit with awards ceremony

### Community & Belonging
- **Exclusive Ambassador Groups**: Private Slack/Discord channels by region
- **Networking Events**: Quarterly regional meetups for peer connections
- **National Summit**: Annual in-person gathering for all ambassadors
- **Mentor Program**: Pair experienced ambassadors with new ones

### Professional Development
- **Free Training**: Leadership workshops, communication clinics, event planning masterclasses
- **Certification Programs**: Recognized credentials in community management
- **Speaking Opportunities**: Local and national speaking engagements
- **Career Connections**: Networking with industry leaders and potential employers

### Influence & Voice
- **Feedback Committees**: Input on product decisions and program direction
- **Advisory Board**: Top ambassadors serve on quarterly advisory councils
- **Content Control**: Feature their ideas in program communications
- **Decision Participation**: Vote on program improvements and initiatives

### Access & Experiences
- **Early Feature Access**: Try new community features before public launch
- **Exclusive Content**: Ambassador-only resources, training materials
- **Event Passes**: Free or discounted access to tournaments, workshops, seminars
- **Product Discounts**: Special pricing on community products and services

## Performance Metrics & Tracking

### Measurement Framework

**Recruitment Performance**
- New members recruited per month (target: 5-10 for Club Ambassadors)
- Quality of recruits (active retention after 30 days)
- Diversity of recruitment channels

**Event Performance**
- Number of events hosted per month (target: 1-2)
- Attendance rates and participant satisfaction
- Event quality ratings (feedback survey)
- Repeat attendee rates

**Community Engagement**
- Feedback rating on ambassador performance (1-5 star scale)
- Retention rate of members recruited by ambassador
- Participation in program-sponsored activities
- Contribution of content (photos, testimonials, reviews)

**Regional Coordinator Specific**
- Support quality for club ambassadors
- Cross-club event coordination success
- Ambassador retention rates under their support
- Regional growth and expansion

**Program Ambassador Specific**
- Social media impressions generated
- Event attendance and speaking engagement
- Content contribution quality and frequency
- Brand partnership facilitation

### Tracking System

- **Monthly Reporting**: Ambassadors submit metrics via portal (recruitment, events, attendance)
- **Automated Dashboard**: Real-time tracking of performance metrics
- **Quarterly Reviews**: PM conducts performance reviews with each ambassador
- **Annual Assessment**: Comprehensive evaluation for top performer designation

## Payment & Distribution Process

### Monthly Bonuses
- **Calculation**: Automatic based on performance metrics
- **Payment Schedule**: Distributed via direct deposit on the 1st of following month
- **Minimum Threshold**: Must achieve 90%+ performance to qualify
- **Verification**: PM reviews metrics before processing

### Quarterly & Annual Awards
- **Selection Process**: PM identifies top performers based on quarterly reviews
- **Notification**: Winners notified 1 week before announcement
- **Distribution**: Paid within 5 business days of formal announcement
- **Public Announcement**: Featured in newsletter, social media, and annual summit

### Non-Monetary Rewards
- **Swag Shipment**: Monthly, with personalized ambassador welcome note
- **Access Activation**: Immediately upon qualification (group adds, feature access)
- **Event Invitations**: Sent 2-3 weeks before event
- **Recognition Rollout**: Monthly newsletter cycles, quarterly announcements

## Incentive Philosophy & Guardrails

### What We Reward
✅ Recruitment quality (not just quantity)  
✅ Consistent performance (not one-time spikes)  
✅ Community building (not just individual metrics)  
✅ Feedback and improvement (learning mindset)  
✅ Mentorship and peer support  
✅ Professional development progress  

### What We Don't Reward
❌ Unsustainable growth (burnout risk)  
❌ Unethical practices (quality over shortcuts)  
❌ Non-compliance with community guidelines  
❌ Negative feedback or community complaints  
❌ Inactive participation (<50% engagement)  

### Adjustment Policies
- **Quarterly Review**: Program adjusts incentive levels based on budget and performance data
- **Feedback Loop**: Ambassadors can request incentive adjustments through formal channels
- **Equity Checks**: PM reviews incentive distribution quarterly to ensure fairness
- **Budget Protection**: If costs exceed budget, bonuses scale down proportionally (not eliminated)

---

## Contingency Scenarios

### If Recruitment Exceeds Target
- Increase Tier 2 recognition pool (more ambassadors qualify)
- Expand non-monetary benefits (more events, training, access)
- Activate stretch bonuses for exceptional performance
- Maintain budget discipline (proportional scaling)

### If Budget Pressure Occurs
- Prioritize Tier 1 monthly bonuses (all ambassadors maintain baseline)
- Reduce Tier 3 annual awards rather than Tier 2 quarterly
- Increase non-monetary benefits (cost-effective)
- Communicate transparently with ambassadors

### If Retention Drops
- Increase Tier 1 recognition (spotlight underperforming cohorts)
- Add professional development benefits
- Create ambassador feedback committee
- Individual outreach and support from PM
