## Process Notes

### Pre-Call Research (Aug 23-25)
For each club:
1. Verify contact person and phone/email
2. Research facility details (courts, membership)
3. Identify strategic initiatives
4. Map decision-making structure
5. Note 2-3 preliminary opportunities
6. Prepare specific questions

### Discovery Call (Aug 26-30)
Follow the framework:
1. Build rapport (5 min)
2. Understand their context (15-20 min)
3. Explore partnership opportunities (15-20 min)
4. Share preliminary thoughts (5-10 min)
5. Confirm next steps (3-5 min)

### Post-Call Documentation (Same Day)
1. Complete call notes
2. Document opportunities
3. Assign fit score (1-5)
4. Note any concerns
5. Update research file
6. Send thank-you/follow-up email

## Troubleshooting

**Club won't answer calls**:
- Try multiple contact methods (phone, email, LinkedIn)
- Ask for referrals from other contacts
- Consider extending timeline to early Sept

**Limited information available**:
- Use discovery call to fill gaps
- Ask club directly about strategic priorities
- Gather info through the conversation, not just research

**Fit assessment unclear**:
- Re-review call notes
- Ask clarifying questions in follow-up email
- Focus on decision timeline and budget signals
- When in doubt, aim for 3+ clubs with high fit

---

## Notes & Tips

✅ **Do**:
- Be genuinely curious; listen more than pitch
- Take detailed notes during calls
- Follow up within 24 hours
- Reference specific details from conversation
- Build rapport and trust

❌ **Don't**:
- Oversell early
- Make commitments without authority
- Ignore red flags or poor fit signals
- Go silent between phases
- Assume fit without validation
