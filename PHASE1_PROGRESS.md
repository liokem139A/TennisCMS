## Key Risks & Mitigation

### Risk 1: Low Discovery Call Completion Rate
- **Risk**: Prospects don't respond to outreach
- **Mitigation**: Use warm introductions, multi-channel outreach, flexible scheduling
- **Contingency**: Extend outreach timeline, broaden prospect list

### Risk 2: Budget Mismatch
- **Risk**: Market budgets don't match assumptions ($5k-$250k)
- **Mitigation**: Discovery calls calibrate expectations, packages remain flexible
- **Contingency**: Adjust package pricing or create additional tiers

### Risk 3: Weak Category Fit
- **Risk**: Certain sponsor categories don't align with community
- **Mitigation**: Validate positioning in discovery calls early
- **Contingency**: Pivot to strongest categories, reduce Phase 2 scope

### Risk 4: Timeline Slippage
- **Risk**: Discovery calls extend beyond Sept 2
- **Mitigation**: Front-load scheduling, parallel execution, backup prospects
- **Contingency**: Phase 2 begins Sept 6 regardless; synthesis continues
