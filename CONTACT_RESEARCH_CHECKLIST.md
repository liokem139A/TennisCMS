## Research Tools & Resources

### Primary Tools
- **LinkedIn Premium** (recommended for advanced searches)
  - Used for: Finding specific contacts by title/company
  - Time per search: 5 minutes
  - Cost: ~$40/month if not already subscribed

- **Google Search** (free)
  - Syntax: "company name" + "job title" + "email"
  - Example: "Wilson Sporting Goods" "Sponsorship Manager" "email"

- **Company Websites**
  - Check "Contact Us" pages for department contacts
  - Look for sponsorship/partnership inquiry forms

- **Local Search** (Google Maps, Yelp)
  - Used for: Finding local businesses and direct contacts
  - Time per search: 5-10 minutes

### Advanced Techniques
- **Email Format Discovery**: Check multiple LinkedIn profiles from same company to identify email pattern
- **LinkedIn Company Pages**: Sort employees by job title to find specific roles
- **Press Release Search**: Company press releases often name partnership contacts
- **LinkedIn Outreach**: If you find someone in the right department, they may know the specific person

### Backup Strategy if Contact Not Found
If you cannot find a specific contact for a prospect:
1. Use general "partnerships" or "sponsorships" email from company website
2. Use LinkedIn message to reach any person in relevant department
3. Call main company line and ask for department transfer
4. Document in notes: "Used general partnerships email until direct contact found"
