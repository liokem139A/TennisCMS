### Key Design Decisions

**Ambassador Roles & Compensation**:
- **Club Ambassador** (40 target): Local champion, event host, community builder
  - Base: $50-100/month + quarterly/annual bonuses
- **Regional Coordinator** (8-10 target): Multi-club leader, regional events, recruiting manager
  - Base: $75-125/month + quarterly/annual bonuses
- **Program Ambassador/Influencer** (5-10 target): High-visibility, social media, brand advocacy
  - Base: $100-150/month + quarterly/annual bonuses

**Incentive Structure**:
- Tiered monthly bonuses: $50-100 (Club), $75-125 (Regional), $100-150 (Program)
- Quarterly performance bonuses: $200-500 for top 25% performers
- Annual recognition: $1,000-2,000 for top 10% performers
- **Annual budget for 75 ambassadors: $45,000-60,000**
- Non-monetary benefits: Recognition, early access to features, community, professional development

**Recruitment Strategy**:
- **5 channels**: Club partnerships (40%), community referrals (20%), coaching networks (15%), social media (15%), events (10%)
- **Timeline**: Sept 6-25 active recruitment, Sept 26-30 onboarding
- **Target**: 50-100 ambassadors with diversity across geographies, demographics, backgrounds

**Training & Onboarding**:
- 3-hour structured curriculum (5 modules)
- 4-week post-training buddy system and check-ins
- Monthly ambassador calls (1st Friday)
- Quarterly regional meetups
- Ongoing support through Slack/Discord community
- FAQ and resource library access

**Portal Specifications**:
- **MVP Features**: Authentication, dashboard, events, resources, reporting
- **Phase 2 Features**: Leaderboard, community chat, analytics, social integration
- **Technology**: React frontend, Node.js/Express backend, PostgreSQL database, AWS cloud, REST API
- **Timeline**: MVP staging by Sept 1, production by Sept 6
