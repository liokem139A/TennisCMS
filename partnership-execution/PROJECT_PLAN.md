## Risks & Mitigation

### Risk 1: Call Scheduling Challenges
- **Risk**: Clubs unavailable during Aug 26-30
- **Mitigation**: Begin outreach immediately; offer flexible times including early morning/evening
- **Fallback**: Extend call window to Sept 5 if needed (adjust timeline)

### Risk 2: Incomplete Research
- **Risk**: Limited information available on some clubs
- **Mitigation**: Use discovery call to fill gaps; lead with listening not assumptions
- **Contingency**: Gather research as calls happen; refine proposals based on actual conversations

### Risk 3: Poor Fit Identification
- **Risk**: Pursuing partnerships that aren't actually viable
- **Mitigation**: Use fit scoring (1-5) and decision criteria to identify true opportunities early
- **Decision Point**: Only develop proposals for clubs with fit score 4+ (high potential)

### Risk 4: Timeline Compression
- **Risk**: Sept 1-15 window tight for proposal development
- **Mitigation**: Start proposal drafting in parallel (late Aug) based on preliminary call findings
- **Buffer**: Prioritize top 3 clubs first; expand if capacity allows
