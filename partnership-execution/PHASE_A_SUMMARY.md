## Why This Approach Works

### 1. Structured but Flexible
- Provides clear guardrails (agenda, timeline, documentation)
- Allows natural conversation flow and relationship building
- Adapts to each club's unique situation during calls

### 2. Research-Informed Conversations
- Pre-call research ensures smart, informed questions
- Shows respect for club's time and priorities
- Identifies opportunities before the call
- Enables credible, strategic positioning

### 3. Consistency & Comparison
- Standardized templates allow easy comparison across 5 clubs
- Fit scoring makes prioritization objective
- Enables pattern recognition (common opportunities across clubs)

### 4. Proposal Efficiency
- Post-call research directly feeds proposal templates
- Discovery call findings directly reference in proposals
- No wasted effort creating generic proposals
- Customization is built in from the start

### 5. Relationship Foundation
- Genuine listening vs. selling creates trust
- Follow-up emails reinforce key themes from calls
- Proposals feel responsive to actual needs (because they are)
- Sets stage for real partnership discussions, not sales pitches

## Critical Success Factors

### 1. Research Quality
- Spend the full 3-5 hours researching each club
- Go beyond basic info to understand strategic context
- Identify stakeholders and decision-making process
- Pre-identify 2-3 potential opportunities

### 2. Call Quality
- Actually listen - don't just pitch
- Ask follow-up questions when unclear
- Take detailed notes you can reference later
- Build genuine rapport, not transactional connection

### 3. Timely Follow-up
- Send thank you email within 24 hours
- Reference specific things they said
- Confirm proposal timeline (typically Sept 1-10)
- Answer any follow-up questions quickly

### 4. Honest Fit Assessment
- Don't force proposals for poor fits
- Use 1-5 scoring system rigorously
- Only develop proposals for 4-5 scores
- Better to focus on 3 excellent fits than 5 mediocre ones

### 5. Customization
- Every proposal must feel specific to that club
- Reference discovery call findings explicitly
- Address their stated goals, not just our offerings
- Avoid anything that feels like copy-paste
