# Discovery Call Framework - Tier 1 Club Partnership Initiative

**Objective**: Understand each club's organizational needs, goals, and partnership opportunities to develop tailored partnership proposals.

**Timeline**: Aug 26-30, 2026 (5 discovery calls)

**Target Clubs**: 
- VTA (Venetian Tennis Academy)
- HCTF (High Country Tennis Foundation)
- HTC (Heritage Tennis Club)
- SGC (Sunnyvale Golf & Tennis Club)
- DTC (Downtown Tennis Complex)

---

## Call Structure & Duration

- **Duration**: 45-60 minutes
- **Format**: 1:1 phone/video call with club decision-maker
- **Timing**: Schedule during non-peak hours (typically early morning or after 5 PM)
- **Attendees**: Business Development Lead + Club Director/Manager

## Call Agenda (45-60 minutes)

### Opening (5 minutes)
- **Rapport Building**: Brief personal/professional connection
- **Purpose Framing**: "We're exploring how we can partner with high-performing clubs like yours to create value for your members and programs"
- **Agenda Walkthrough**: "I'd like to understand your club better, then share some preliminary thoughts on where we might create opportunities together"

### Club Context Discovery (15-20 minutes)

#### Organizational Structure & Leadership
- How long has your club been operating?
- What is your current membership size and composition?
- What are your main program offerings?
- Who makes strategic decisions about new partnerships or initiatives?

#### Strategic Goals & Challenges
- What are your top 1-3 strategic priorities for the next 12-24 months?
- What operational or revenue challenges are you facing?
- How is your club positioned relative to competitors in your market?
- Are there specific segments of members you're trying to grow?

#### Current Programs & Capabilities
- What are your strongest programs or differentiators?
- How do members currently learn about new opportunities or programs?
- What partnerships or vendor relationships do you currently have?
- Where do you see gaps in your current offerings?

### Partnership Exploration (15-20 minutes)

#### Value Drivers
- What would make a partnership feel valuable to your club?
- Are there specific pain points you'd want a partner to help solve?
- What does success look like for your club in a partnership?

#### Decision-Making Process
- What is your decision-making timeline for new initiatives?
- Who else would need to be involved in evaluating a partnership?
- Are there budget allocations already set aside for partnerships?
- What does your typical approval process look like?

#### Competitive Landscape
- Are you already in conversations with other potential partners?
- What would differentiate a partnership approach for you?

### Preliminary Opportunity Discussion (5-10 minutes)

- Share 2-3 high-level opportunities you've identified based on conversation
- Gauge initial reaction and interest level
- Ask clarifying questions on feasibility
- Do NOT make commitments; frame as "exploratory"

### Closing & Next Steps (3-5 minutes)

- Summarize key themes and opportunities
- Propose timeline for follow-up (custom proposal delivery, next conversation)
- Thank them for their time and insights
- Confirm contact info and preferred communication method

---

## Key Listening Points

**During the call, listen for:**

1. **Urgency Signals**: Are they facing immediate challenges that align with our offerings?
2. **Decision Timeline**: How quickly can they move on new initiatives?
3. **Budget Reality**: Do they have budget flexibility or is everything allocated?
4. **Stakeholder Dynamics**: Who is the real decision-maker? Any politics to navigate?
5. **Emotional Tone**: What creates excitement vs. skepticism? Where's the enthusiasm?
6. **Hidden Opportunities**: Problems they mention tangentially might be their biggest pain points

## Notes & Tips

- **Be Curious, Not Salesy**: Listen more than you pitch. The goal is to understand, not convert.
- **Take Notes**: Show you're engaged; reference notes in follow-up; demonstrates professionalism.
- **Ask Follow-ups**: If something isn't clear, ask. Don't assume.
- **Validate Assumptions**: "It sounds like you're really focused on X. Have I understood correctly?"
- **Build Rapport**: Reference something specific about their club if possible.
- **End with Excitement**: Leave them wanting the follow-up conversation.
