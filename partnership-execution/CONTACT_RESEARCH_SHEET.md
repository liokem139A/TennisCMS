## Quick Research Guide

**Primary Sources**:
1. Club website (Contact Us, Leadership, Team pages)
2. LinkedIn (club page + key executive searches)
3. Google Business listing (name, phone, address)
4. Local news (recent announcements, new leaders)
5. Industry directories / Chamber of Commerce
6. Direct phone call to club main line

**When You Find Contact Info**:
1. Note the source
2. Verify the email/phone is current
3. Try to find direct line (not just main club number)
4. Look for LinkedIn profile
5. Check if there's a secondary contact (board member, assistant)

## Research Tips

### When Finding Contact Info on Club Website:
✅ Look for these pages: Contact Us, Team, Leadership, Management  
✅ LinkedIn profiles often have direct email formats  
✅ If you see "info@club.com", search for direct person's email  
✅ Check domain email (usually firstname@clubdomain.com pattern)  

### When Calling Main Club Number:
✅ "Hello, I'm trying to reach the Executive Director/GM. Can you give me their direct line?"  
✅ Ask for: "Who is the primary contact for partnership discussions?"  
✅ Verify spelling of name and email address  
✅ Ask best time to reach them and preferred communication  

### When Using LinkedIn:
✅ Search "[Club Name]" on LinkedIn to find official club page  
✅ Look at club page - it often lists current leadership  
✅ Search "[Club Name] Executive Director" for current leadership  
✅ Check if person's LinkedIn lists direct phone or prefers email  

### Red Flags (Verify Carefully):
❌ Outdated info (check when last updated)  
❌ Old phone numbers (call to verify current)  
❌ Generic emails (try to find direct contact)  
❌ Multiple people with same title (find right decision-maker)
