## Key Research Tips

### Finding Executive Director/General Manager
**On website look for**:
- "About Us" → "Leadership" or "Team"
- "Contact" page with staff list
- "Board of Directors" (for non-profits)
- Site footer with email addresses

**LinkedIn search pattern**:
- Search: "[Club Name]" to find official page
- Check "Current employees" section
- Search: "[Club Name] Executive Director" or "General Manager"
- Look at person's profile for contact info or LinkedIn message option

**Google Business pattern**:
- Search: "[Club Name] [City]"
- Check Google Business listing for phone
- Call and ask: "May I speak with the General Manager?"
- Ask: "What's the best email for partnership inquiries?"

**Phone call script**:
> "Hi, I'm trying to reach the Executive Director/General Manager at [Club Name]. Is this the right number? Could you give me their direct line or email?"
