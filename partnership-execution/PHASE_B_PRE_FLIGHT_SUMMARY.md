## If You Get Stuck

### Can't Find Contact Info?
- Try club website → "Contact Us" or "Leadership" page
- Search LinkedIn for club name + "Executive Director"
- Call main club number and ask for leadership contact
- Check recent news articles (often have quotes from leadership)

### Club Not Responding to Email?
- Call directly on phone (call is better than 5th email)
- Leave professional voicemail with brief description
- Send follow-up email referencing your call
- Try afternoon/evening if morning doesn't work

### Need to Reschedule a Call?
- It's OK - work around their schedule
- No call gets missed - reschedule to early Sept if needed
- Document any changes in CALL_SCHEDULING_TRACKER.md
- Send confirmation email immediately when rescheduled

### Uncertain About Fit for a Club?
- Ask them during discovery call
- Your job is to understand, not judge
- Let the discovery call findings inform fit assessment
- More info = better proposal (if you advance to Phase C)
