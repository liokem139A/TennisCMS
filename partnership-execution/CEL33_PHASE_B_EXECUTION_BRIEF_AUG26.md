## DISCOVERY CALL TALKING POINTS (Use for All 5 Calls)

### OPENING (5 min)
```
"Hi [Name], thanks so much for taking the time to chat today. I'm Tao Nguyen, 
Business Development Lead at Celadon Tennis. We're building the leading digital 
tennis community and tournament management platform in Vietnam, and we're looking 
to partner with leading tennis clubs and facilities like [Club Name] to deliver 
better experiences for players and organizers.

Today, I'd love to learn more about your club, your strategic priorities, and 
explore whether there might be areas where we could work together. This is just 
an exploratory conversation—no pressure. Sound good?"
```

### DISCOVERY PHASE (20 min) — ASK THESE QUESTIONS

**Club Strategy & Priorities**:
1. "What are your top 2-3 strategic priorities for the next 12-24 months?"
2. "How do you currently measure success with your member experience?"
3. "What are the biggest challenges you face in managing tournaments or events?"

**Current Programs & Partnerships**:
4. "Can you walk me through your current tournament offerings and member programs?"
5. "Do you have any existing technology partnerships or management tools in place?"
6. "What's working well with your current setup, and what could be improved?"

**Partnership Fit Assessment**:
7. "Are there specific areas where you'd want partner support (technology, operations, marketing)?"
8. "What would an ideal partnership look like from your perspective?"
9. "What's your timeline for evaluating or implementing new tools/partnerships?"

**Champions & Decision-Making**:
10. "Beyond you, who else would typically be involved in decisions about new partnerships?"
11. "If we developed a proposal tailored to [Club Name]'s needs, who would need to approve it?"

### EXPLORATION PHASE (10 min) — PRESENT VALUE PROP

```
"Based on what you've shared, I think there could be real value in a partnership. 
Here's what I'm seeing:

[Based on their input, explain 2-3 specific ways Celadon could help]
- Platform feature A addresses [their challenge]
- Operational feature B helps with [their priority]
- Community feature C aligns with [their goal]

I'm thinking we could put together a custom proposal that addresses your specific 
needs. Would you be open to us exploring that?"
```

### CLOSING (5-10 min) — LOCK NEXT STEPS

```
"Great, I'd like to put together a proposal tailored to [Club Name]'s specific needs 
and timeline. 

Here's what I'm thinking:
- I'll send you a draft proposal by [DATE] that addresses the priorities you mentioned
- We can schedule a follow-up call in 1 week to review the proposal and answer questions
- If there's real fit, we'd move toward discussing terms and timeline

Does that timeline work for you? And is [Email] the best way to get you the proposal?"
```
