## Prospect Research Framework

Each prospect should be evaluated on:
1. **Budget Fit**: Can they afford our packages ($5k-$250k+)?
2. **Alignment**: Does sponsorship fit their brand/business model?
3. **Reach Overlap**: Do they reach our target audience?
4. **Decision Timeline**: When do they make sponsorship decisions?
5. **Relationship Readiness**: Are we known? Cold outreach or warm intro?
6. **Strategic Value**: Is this a strategic partnership or transactional?
