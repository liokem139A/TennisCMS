## 📞 TIPS FOR SUCCESSFUL RESEARCH

### LinkedIn Research Tips
1. **Use advanced search**: "People who work at [company]" + "VP" or "Director"
2. **Check multiple profiles**: If first person doesn't have contact info, try the next
3. **Read About sections**: Often mention email or contact preferences
4. **Look at recent changes**: People who recently changed title = more open to partnerships
5. **Check if they're "Open to work"**: Signals receptiveness to partnerships

### Email Identification Tips
1. **Check company website** for email format: usually firstname.lastname@domain.com
2. **Use free tools**: hunter.io (email finder) or clearbit (shows work emails)
3. **Try common formats**: 
   - firstname.lastname@company.com
   - first.last@company.com
   - firstl@company.com
4. **Look at LinkedIn signature**: Many profiles show email directly
5. **Check company "Contact Us"**: May have general partnership email

### Phone Number Tips
1. **LinkedIn profile**: Check "Contact info" section
2. **Company website**: Check "Contact Us" page
3. **Company directory**: Some companies have searchable staff directories
4. **Local research**: Direct call to company switchboard, ask for extension
