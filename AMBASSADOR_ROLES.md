# Ambassador Roles & Responsibilities - Detailed Guide

**Purpose**: Define detailed role expectations, qualifications, and success metrics for each ambassador tier  
**Audience**: Recruiters, ambassadors, program leadership  
**Last Updated**: August 23, 2026

---

## Role 1: Club Ambassador

### Overview
Club ambassadors are the frontline representatives in their tennis clubs. They drive recruitment, engagement, and retention within their specific club community.

### Role Qualifications

**Required**
- Active club member or regular participant (6+ months)
- Beginner to advanced tennis skill level (no level too high or low)
- Lives or works within 15 miles of assigned club
- Available 5-8 hours per month
- Access to reliable internet and smartphone

**Strongly Preferred**
- Previous volunteer or leadership experience
- Social media proficiency
- Event planning experience
- Strong personal network at club
- Positive reputation and trusted by club community

**Disqualifying Factors**
- History of disciplinary issues at club
- Conflicts of interest with club management
- Unable to commit to 6-month term (minimum)
- Language barriers preventing clear communication

### Primary Responsibilities

#### Community Recruitment
- Identify and recruit 5-10 new members per month
- Conduct outreach through in-person conversations, phone, email, social media
- Share program benefits and clarify expectations
- Collect contact information and pass qualified leads to program
- Follow up with prospects and encourage participation
- Track recruitment pipeline and conversion rates

#### Member Engagement & Retention
- Organize and host 1-2 club activities per month (e.g., Tuesday night socials, weekend tournaments, skill clinics)
- Promote events through multiple channels (email, text, in-person, social media)
- Ensure events are inclusive and welcoming for all skill levels
- Capture attendance and feedback from events
- Follow up with non-attendees to understand barriers
- Create sense of community and belonging

#### Community Intelligence & Feedback
- Conduct monthly surveys or casual polls with members
- Gather feedback on program, events, and member experience
- Identify pain points and opportunities for improvement
- Report trends and insights to regional coordinator
- Escalate concerns or complaints appropriately
- Serve as voice of community to program leadership

#### Metric Reporting & Accountability
- Submit monthly performance report by 5th of month
- Track recruitment numbers, event attendance, engagement metrics
- Maintain accurate records in ambassador portal
- Share case studies or stories of successful member experiences
- Celebrate wins and highlight contributions

#### Professional Representation
- Attend quarterly training and certification programs
- Participate in monthly community calls
- Represent program professionally in all interactions
- Follow brand guidelines and communication standards
- Maintain confidentiality of member data
- Act as positive role model in club

### Success Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| New members recruited | 5-10/month | Monthly |
| Events hosted | 1-2/month | Monthly |
| Event attendance rate | 80%+ | Monthly |
| Member satisfaction | 4.5+ stars | Quarterly |
| Retention rate (members stay) | 85%+ | Quarterly |
| Training completion | 100% | On-demand |
| Report submission | 100% on time | Monthly |
| Community feedback score | 4.5+ | Monthly |

### Time Commitment Breakdown

| Activity | Hours/Month |
|----------|------------|
| Recruitment outreach | 1.5-2 |
| Event planning & hosting | 2-3 |
| Communications & follow-up | 1-1.5 |
| Reporting & admin | 0.5-1 |
| Training & development | 0.5-1 |
| **Total** | **5-8** |

### Compensation & Benefits

**Monthly**
- $50-100 performance bonus (90%+ performance)
- Branded swag (rotating: hats, shirts, water bottles)
- 15-20% discount on merchandise
- Digital badge and leaderboard recognition

**Quarterly (Top 25%)**
- $200-500 bonus for exceptional performance
- Premium branded gear (jackets, tech items)
- Feature in program newsletter/social media
- Exclusive event invitations

**Annual (Top 10%)**
- $1,000+ annual recognition bonus
- "Ambassador of the Year" award
- Premium experience (coaching, events, travel)
- Speaking opportunity at annual summit

### Support & Resources

- Dedicated program coordinator (email + monthly 1-on-1 calls)
- Private ambassador Slack channel
- Template library (emails, flyers, social posts)
- Activity ideas and best practices library
- Monthly training and development sessions
- Access to member database and CRM tools
- Event planning support and guidance

### Onboarding & Training

**Initial Onboarding (3 hours)**
- 30 min: 1-on-1 call with program coordinator
- 1 hour: Online modules (community vision, brand voice, portal)
- 1 hour: Role-specific training (recruitment, event planning)
- 30 min: Q&A and resource walkthrough

**Ongoing Development (6 hours/year)**
- Monthly 1-hour group training calls
- Quarterly in-person regional meetups
- Access to online learning library
- Certification programs (optional)

## Role 2: Regional Coordinator

### Overview
Regional coordinators oversee club ambassadors in a geographic region (typically 2-3 clubs). They provide training, support, coordination, and escalation for their territory.

### Role Qualifications

**Required**
- 1+ year experience in ambassador program or similar role
- Demonstrated leadership and management skills
- Responsible for 2-3 club ambassadors minimum
- Available 10-12 hours per month
- Strong organizational and communication skills
- Reliable internet access and professional communication tools

**Strongly Preferred**
- Previous management or supervisory experience
- Deep knowledge of regional tennis landscape
- Project management experience
- Conflict resolution and negotiation skills
- Data analysis and reporting proficiency

**Disqualifying Factors**
- Unable to manage interpersonal conflicts professionally
- Inconsistent with program standards
- Unavailable for required regional activities
- Poor communication or follow-through history

### Primary Responsibilities

#### Ambassador Management & Support
- Recruit, onboard, and train club ambassadors
- Conduct monthly 1-on-1 check-ins with each ambassador
- Provide coaching, mentoring, and performance feedback
- Address performance issues and develop improvement plans
- Recognize high performers and celebrate wins
- Maintain ambassador motivation and reduce churn
- Escalate issues to program leadership appropriately

#### Cross-Club Coordination
- Organize quarterly cross-club events (tournaments, socials, clinics)
- Coordinate ambassador meetings and training sessions
- Share best practices and learnings across clubs
- Facilitate collaboration and peer support
- Identify and resolve inter-club conflicts or issues
- Build community among ambassadors

#### Performance Monitoring & Reporting
- Track ambassador metrics and KPIs
- Create monthly regional performance dashboard
- Identify trends and opportunities for improvement
- Report monthly progress to program leadership
- Prepare regional case studies and impact stories
- Conduct quarterly performance reviews

#### Program Quality & Compliance
- Ensure all ambassadors follow brand guidelines
- Monitor community feedback and sentiment
- Address quality issues or brand misrepresentation
- Conduct spot-checks of events and activities
- Maintain high standards of program integrity
- Document and resolve member complaints

#### Budget & Resource Management
- Manage regional event budget and expenses
- Track and optimize program spending
- Request resources needed for ambassadors
- Coordinate swag distribution and ordering
- Manage equipment or materials for events
- Report regional financial metrics

### Success Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Ambassadors managed | 2-3 ambassadors | Ongoing |
| Ambassador retention rate | 90%+ | Quarterly |
| Average ambassador performance score | 4.5+ | Quarterly |
| Cross-club events | 1/quarter | Quarterly |
| Report submission | 100% on time | Monthly |
| Regional member satisfaction | 4.6+ stars | Quarterly |
| Escalation resolution time | <5 days | Ongoing |
| Training completion (all ambassadors) | 100% | Quarterly |

### Time Commitment Breakdown

| Activity | Hours/Month |
|----------|------------|
| Ambassador 1-on-1 meetings | 2-3 |
| Cross-club event planning | 2-2.5 |
| Performance monitoring & reporting | 2-3 |
| Ambassador training & development | 1.5-2 |
| Communication & coordination | 1.5-2 |
| Admin & documentation | 1-1.5 |
| **Total** | **10-12** |

### Compensation & Benefits

**Monthly**
- $75-125 performance bonus (90%+ performance)
- Premium branded swag
- 20% discount on merchandise
- Recognition and leaderboard ranking

**Quarterly (Top 25%)**
- $300-600 bonus for exceptional performance
- Premium branded gear and tech items
- Speaking opportunity at regional events
- Feature in program marketing

**Annual (Top 10%)**
- $1,500+ annual recognition bonus
- Potential path to Senior Coordinator or Program Leadership role
- Professional development budget
- Conference or industry event attendance

### Support & Resources

- Dedicated program manager (weekly check-ins)
- Regional coordinator peer network
- Advanced ambassador Slack channel
- Full access to program reporting and analytics
- Event planning and execution support
- Budget and resource management tools
- Leadership training and development programs

### Reporting Structure

- Reports to: Senior Program Manager or Program Director
- Manages: 2-3 Club Ambassadors
- Peer network: Other Regional Coordinators (4-6 peers)
- Escalation: Program Director for major decisions

## Role 3: Program Ambassador (Influencer)

### Overview
Program ambassadors are high-visibility individuals who promote the program, build credibility, and attract top talent through their influence and reputation.

### Role Qualifications

**Required**
- Well-known in tennis community (coach, tournament director, content creator, athlete)
- Strong social media presence (minimum 500+ followers relevant to tennis)
- Available 3-5 hours per month
- Professional reputation and credibility
- Willing to be public face of program

**Strongly Preferred**
- 10,000+ social media followers
- Regular speaking or media appearances
- Previous sponsorship or brand ambassador experience
- Strong personal brand aligned with program values
- Access to events and high-profile networks

**Disqualifying Factors**
- Controversial reputation or polarizing views
- Competing sponsorships or loyalty issues
- Unreliable or inconsistent performance
- Misalignment with program values or mission

### Primary Responsibilities

#### Program Promotion
- Create and share 2-4 social media posts per month promoting program
- Write blog posts or articles about program and community
- Appear at 2+ program events per quarter
- Give testimonials and endorsements
- Represent program at tennis events and conferences
- Leverage personal network to attract talent

#### Content Creation
- Share ambassador journey and success stories
- Create behind-the-scenes content and photos/videos
- Host webinars or talks about program benefits
- Contribute to program newsletter and communications
- Generate original content ideas and campaigns
- Collaborate with marketing team on initiatives

#### Credibility & Influence
- Serve as program spokesperson for media inquiries
- Provide credibility and validation for program
- Attract high-profile candidates to ambassador program
- Open doors to partnerships and sponsorships
- Build bridges with industry leaders and organizations
- Enhance program reputation and reach

#### Feedback & Insights
- Share member feedback and community insights
- Identify trends and opportunities in market
- Provide perspective on program messaging and positioning
- Suggest improvements and innovations
- Contribute to strategic discussions
- Serve on advisory board (optional)

### Success Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Social media impressions | 500+ / month | Monthly |
| Content pieces created | 2-4 / month | Monthly |
| Program event attendance | 2+ / quarter | Quarterly |
| Testimonial submissions | 2+ / year | As requested |
| Media mentions/features | 2+ / quarter | Quarterly |
| Feedback rating | 4.8+ stars | Quarterly |
| Community engagement | Positive sentiment | Monthly |

### Time Commitment Breakdown

| Activity | Hours/Month |
|----------|------------|
| Social media content | 1-1.5 |
| Event appearances | 1-1.5 |
| Content creation | 0.5-1 |
| Communications | 0.5 |
| **Total** | **3-5** |

### Compensation & Benefits

**Monthly**
- $100-150 stipend or performance bonus
- Premium exclusive swag
- 25% discount on merchandise
- Elevated status and recognition

**Quarterly (Top Contributors)**
- $500-1,000 bonus for exceptional visibility
- Exclusive experiences and opportunities
- Co-marketing opportunities
- Featured speaker at major events

**Annual (Top Tier)**
- $2,000+ annual recognition bonus
- Premium partnership or advisory role
- Co-branded content and campaigns
- Paid speaking engagements
- Sponsorship or partnership opportunities

### Support & Resources

- Dedicated marketing manager liaison
- Swag and materials provided on request
- Early access to program announcements
- Exclusive influencer Slack channel
- Media training and support
- Content creation assistance
- Event coordination and logistics

### Reporting Structure

- Reports to: Marketing Manager or Chief Community Officer
- Network: 5-10 other program ambassadors
- Escalation: Chief Community Officer or Executive Team

## Performance Improvement & Exits

### Performance Issues

**Underperformance (< 70% of targets)**
- Month 1: Coaching conversation and support
- Month 2: Written performance plan with specific goals
- Month 3: Review and either improvement or transition plan

**Conduct or Brand Issues**
- First offense: Coaching and clarification
- Second offense: Formal warning and retraining
- Third offense: Probation or termination

**Consistent Excellence (> 120% of targets)**
- Promotion opportunity to higher tier
- Leadership role and mentoring responsibilities
- Recognition and increased compensation
- Path to program leadership team

### Ambassador Transitions

**Positive Exit**
- Retirement due to life circumstances
- Promotion to leadership position
- Alumni status with ongoing recognition
- Re-recruitment opportunity in future

**Managed Exit**
- Performance issues not resolved
- Conduct violations
- Misalignment with program values
- Mutual decision to part ways
