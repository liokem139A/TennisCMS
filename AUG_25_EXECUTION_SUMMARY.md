## CONTINGENCY PLANS

### If Calendly Setup Fails
- **Fallback**: Use email time options only (no Calendly link)
- **Alternative**: Google Calendar links or Outlook meeting invites
- **Timeline**: Get functioning within 1 hour or proceed without

### If Prospect Email Bounces
- **Step 1**: Search LinkedIn for alternative contact or email
- **Step 2**: Try company main line → ask for department direct
- **Step 3**: Mark prospect as "email unreachable" in tracking
- **Step 4**: Document attempt and move to follow-up

### If No Response After 5 Days
- **Step 1 (Day 5)**: Send follow-up email (template in OUTREACH_EMAILS_FINAL.md)
- **Step 2 (Day 10)**: LinkedIn message or alternative contact path
- **Step 3 (Day 15)**: Mark for Sept 3-5 follow-up window

### If Discovery Call No-Show
- **Immediate**: Send "missed you" email with rescheduling options
- **Follow-up**: Try calling 15 minutes after scheduled time
- **Reschedule**: Offer 2-3 alternative times
- **Documentation**: Log in EMAIL_TRACKING.md & DISCOVERY_CALL_TRACKER.md
