## Risk Management

**High-Risk Items**:
1. **Low email response rate** → Mitigation: Warm intros, LinkedIn connection, phone follow-up
2. **Prospects don't schedule** → Mitigation: Phone outreach, multiple attempts, flexible timing
3. **Call cancellations** → Mitigation: 24-hr reminders, buffer time, immediate reschedule
4. **Budget misalignment** → Mitigation: Present flexible options, validate early
5. **Low interest signals** → Mitigation: Refine messaging, focus on strongest fit prospects

**Contingency Plans**:
- Expand prospect list to 15-20 if response rate low
- Offer shorter discovery format (20-min phone) if 45-min doesn't work
- Shift focus to strongest prospects if limited interest
- Adjust package tiers to match market reality if needed
