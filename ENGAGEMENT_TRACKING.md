## 2. Engagement Level Categorization (Aug 24-25)

### 2.1 Engagement Tiers

#### Tier 1: High Engagement
- **Indicators**:
  - Opened email multiple times (3+)
  - Clicked multiple links
  - Replied to email with substantive message
  - Requested a meeting
  - Downloaded resources/white papers
- **Action**: Schedule discovery call immediately (within 24 hours)
- **Follow-up**: Personalized call with executive sponsor if available

#### Tier 2: Moderate Engagement
- **Indicators**:
  - Opened email 1-2 times
  - Clicked at least one link
  - No direct reply yet
  - Engagement window: 24-48 hours after send
- **Action**: Personalized follow-up email with specific value prop
- **Follow-up**: Phone call if no response within 48 hours of follow-up

#### Tier 3: Low Engagement
- **Indicators**:
  - Opened email once or not at all
  - No clicks observed
  - No response within 48 hours
  - Engagement window: 48-72 hours after send
- **Action**: Re-engagement email with different angle/offer
- **Follow-up**: Consider alternative contact channel

#### Tier 4: No Engagement (Non-Responders)
- **Indicators**:
  - No open after 72 hours
  - Bounced emails
  - No engagement signals
- **Action**: Multi-channel outreach strategy
- **Follow-up**: LinkedIn outreach, alternative contact info, consider removal

### 2.2 Engagement Scoring System
Create automated scoring based on:
- Open: +10 points
- Click: +25 points
- Reply: +50 points
- Meeting scheduled: +100 points
- **Tier thresholds**:
  - Tier 1: 50+ points
  - Tier 2: 25-49 points
  - Tier 3: 1-24 points
  - Tier 4: 0 points

## 3. Follow-up Sequence Planning (Aug 25-26)

### 3.1 Follow-up Templates by Tier

#### High Engagement (Tier 1) - Immediate Action
**Timeline**: Same day or next business day
```
Subject: Let's Talk [Company Name] Partnership
Body:
- Acknowledge their interest (specific opens/clicks)
- Highlight most relevant benefit based on their engagement
- Propose 3 specific time slots for call
- Include executive summary link
- Personal touch: reference their role/company interest
```

#### Moderate Engagement (Tier 2) - Value Add
**Timeline**: 24 hours after initial send
```
Subject: [Personalized] One Thing You Might Have Missed
Body:
- Reference specific section they engaged with
- Provide additional resource/case study
- Soft call-to-action: "Curious to learn more? Let's chat briefly"
- Time commitment: "Just 15 minutes"
- Alternative: "Do you prefer email or a quick call?"
```

#### Low Engagement (Tier 3) - Re-engagement
**Timeline**: 48 hours after initial send
```
Subject: Is Now a Bad Time? (Different angle)
Body:
- Brief apology/acknowledgment of busy season
- Different value prop than original email
- Specific pain point they might relate to
- Softer ask: "Even 5 min of feedback would help us"
- Exit gracefully: "No pressure, just let me know"
```

#### No Engagement (Tier 4) - Multi-channel
**Timeline**: 72 hours after initial send
- LinkedIn message with personalized note
- Alternative contact (different department if applicable)
- Phone call with gatekeeper strategy
- Decision point: Remove from sequence if no response after this round

### 3.2 Follow-up Sequence Automation
- **Tool**: Automated email sequences in Mailchimp/HubSpot
- **Triggers**: Engagement metrics (open, click, non-response)
- **Delays**: Build in delays between touches
- **Personalization**: Custom fields for company, title, engagement history
- **Opt-out**: Clear unsubscribe mechanism
- **Compliance**: GDPR/CAN-SPAM compliance

## 4. Discovery Call Scheduling Strategy (Aug 27-28)

### 4.1 Call Prioritization
1. **Tier 1 respondents**: Highest priority, schedule within 24 hours
2. **Tier 2 positive responses**: Schedule within 48-72 hours
3. **Tier 3 successful re-engagement**: Schedule as opportunities arise
4. **Tier 4 exceptions**: Only if breakthrough multi-channel response

### 4.2 Call Logistics
- **Duration**: 30-minute discovery call (confirm on invite)
- **Attendees**: Business Development Lead + relevant domain expert
- **Preparation**: Review engagement history, company research, role-specific talking points
- **Goals**:
  - Understand current partnership needs
  - Identify fit/potential collaboration areas
  - Establish next steps (proposal, pilot, etc.)
  - Schedule follow-up action item
- **Recording**: Obtain consent, record for follow-up team
- **Follow-up**: Send notes within 24 hours

### 4.3 Calendar Integration
- **Calendly or similar**: Embed in follow-up emails for easy scheduling
- **Time zones**: Auto-detect and suggest appropriate times
- **Buffer**: 30 min between calls for notes and prep

## 6. Risk Mitigation & Contingencies

### 6.1 Potential Issues
| Issue | Probability | Mitigation |
|-------|------------|-----------|
| Email platform downtime | Low | Have backup email list, manual tracking |
| High bounce rate | Medium | Verify email list quality, clean database first |
| Low open rates | Medium | A/B test subject lines, optimize send times |
| No discovery calls scheduled | High | More aggressive follow-up timing, multi-channel |
| SPAM complaints | Low | Clean list, clear opt-out, compliance review |

### 6.2 Escalation Path
- **Low metrics on Day 1**: Adjust send time, review subject lines
- **Low open rates after Day 2**: Trigger additional outreach channel
- **Zero response rate by Day 4**: Emergency meeting with leadership to adjust strategy
