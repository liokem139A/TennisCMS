## WHAT WE'RE DOING: DISCOVERY CALLS (30-min format)

### Goal
Have authentic conversations with 12 sponsor prospects to:
1. Understand their sponsorship objectives and budget
2. Validate/refine our value propositions
3. Identify 5-8 qualified prospects for Phase 2 (negotiations)

### Discovery Call Structure (30 minutes)
```
Opening (2 min)          → Welcome, agenda, confirmation
Community Intro (3 min)  → Who we are, relevance to them
Their Business (8 min)   → Questions, active listening
Partnership Explore (10 min) → Specific pitch approaches
Closing (4 min)          → Clear next steps
Buffer (3 min)           → Overflow/flexibility time
```

### Key Principle
**Listen 70%, Talk 30%** - The best discovery calls have us asking great questions 
and genuinely understanding the prospect's needs, not giving a hard sell.

## DISCOVERY CALL TALKING POINTS (Key Highlights)

### OPENING (What to say when call starts)
```
"Thanks for taking the time to talk with me! I'm [Your Name] with [Community]. 
On our agenda today - I'd love to learn about you and your priorities, and then 
share how I think we might work together. Does that sound good? Got 30 minutes?"
```

### THEIR BUSINESS (Listen & Learn - Ask these questions)

**Key Questions** (customize by prospect type):
1. "What are your sponsorship/partnership goals for 2026?"
2. "What does success look like for you in a partnership?"
3. "How do you evaluate whether a partnership is working?"
4. "What's your decision-making timeline?"
5. "What's most important to you: brand visibility, community engagement, product placement, or something else?"
6. "Who else needs to be involved in the decision?"
7. "What partnerships have worked really well for you in the past?"

**Listening Tips**:
- Write down their answers - don't interrupt
- Ask follow-up questions ("Tell me more about that...")
- Look for budget signals and decision-maker clues

### PARTNERSHIP EXPLORATION (Our Value Proposition)

**Different angles by prospect type**:

**For Tech Platforms (SwingVision)**:
- "I'm thinking we could integrate your video analysis tool into our community platform..."
- "Would co-branded content or joint marketing around player development be valuable?"
- "Have you explored community partnerships as a way to acquire users?"

**For Equipment Companies (Wilson)**:
- "We could feature Wilson equipment in our tournaments and community content..."
- "Would equipment sponsorship + brand visibility with our engaged members work for you?"
- "We're planning 4-6 tournaments per year - would you want to be featured?"

**For Wellness/Apparel (Lululemon)**:
- "We're looking for partners who share our community-first values..."
- "Would co-hosted wellness events or member discounts be interesting?"
- "Our community aligns with your active lifestyle positioning..."

**For Local Business**:
- "I'm thinking we could help drive foot traffic to your location..."
- "Our members are engaged and looking for local partnerships..."
- "What would be most valuable for you - direct customers, brand visibility, or something else?"

### OBJECTION HANDLING (What to say if they raise concerns)

**Objection 1: "We already have sponsorship programs in place"**
- Response: "I totally understand. I'm curious what's working in those programs and if there's any opportunity to explore something new together. What are your priorities for 2026?"

**Objection 2: "Budget is already committed"**
- Response: "I hear you - most companies plan early. I'm thinking 2027 might be perfect for us. Would it make sense to stay in touch and potentially explore something for early next year?"

**Objection 3: "We're not sure about sponsoring local communities"**
- Response: "That's fair - a lot of big brands are exploring more local partnerships. What if we started small to test it? That way you can see the value without a big commitment."

**Objection 4: "What's our ROI/What do we get?"**
- Response: "Great question. Let me ask you - what's most important to you? [Listen to their answer]. Based on that, here's how I think we could create value..."

**Objection 5: "Who else is involved in this decision?"**
- Response: "That's important to know. Who should we be talking to? [Write down names]. Would it make sense to have them on our next call, or would you rather loop them in first?"

### CLOSING (How to end the call)

**If they're interested**:
```
"Great! So here's what I'm hearing you're interested in [summarize 1-2 key points]. 
Here's what I'm thinking we could explore... [Give 1-2 specific ideas]. 
Next step: I'll send you a proposal outline by [DATE]. Does that work?"
```

**If they're not sure yet**:
```
"I think there's real potential here. Why don't I send you some ideas in writing 
by [DATE], and we can reconnect next week to discuss further?"
```

**If they're not a fit**:
```
"Thanks so much for being honest about this. I really appreciate your time. 
If anything changes next year, I'd love to reconnect."
```

```
- Interest level (1-5 stars): [1-5]

SUCCESS SIGNALS (Did they say any of these?):
- "That's interesting..."
- "I'd like to explore that..."
- "Let me run that by my team..."
- "Send me something in writing..."

RED FLAGS (Anything concerning?):
- No budget
- Wrong contact person
- Not interested in this category
- Timeline too far out

BUDGET CONFIDENCE (1-3 stars):
- 1 star: Budget unclear, hesitant
- 2 stars: Budget mentioned but uncertain
- 3 stars: Budget confirmed, clear signals

NEXT STEPS:
- [ ] Send proposal/materials by [DATE]
- [ ] Follow-up call scheduled? [YES/NO] - [DATE/TIME if yes]
- [ ] Need to contact someone else? [YES/NO] - [Name/info if yes]
- [ ] Thank you email sent? [YES/NO]
```

## TEAM QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

**Q: What if the prospect asks about pricing/packages before we're ready?**
A: "That's great that you're interested! Here's the thing - we want to make sure we 
build the right package for your specific needs. Let's first understand what success 
looks like for you, and then we can talk through what that looks like in terms of 
investment."

**Q: What if they want to schedule a follow-up call with multiple people on their end?**
A: Perfect! That's actually a good sign - means they're interested enough to include 
decision-makers. Coordinate the details and set it up.

**Q: What if they say "we need to think about it" at the end of the call?**
A: "Of course, take the time you need. Here's what I'll do - I'll send you 
[specific materials] by [DATE], and let's reconnect on [DATE] to continue the 
conversation. How does that sound?"

**Q: What if they're clearly not a fit?**
A: "I really appreciate your honesty and your time today. I don't think this is 
the right fit for either of us right now, but I'd love to stay in touch. If 
circumstances change, I'll definitely reach out."
