## ESCALATION PATHS & DECISION RULES

### **BLOCKER ESCALATION** (How to handle when something breaks)

**Level 1: Team → Team (Same-day resolution)**
- **Trigger**: Feature dependency missing, design assets late, lead quality issues
- **Owner**: Respective team lead (Eng, Marketing, Community)
- **Action**: Coordinate directly with other team, find workaround within 2-4 hours
- **Example**: Engineering waiting on design assets → Marketing provides assets or Eng designs placeholder UI

**Level 2: Team → PM (2-4 hour resolution)**
- **Trigger**: Blocker persists 2+ hours, requires resource reallocation, or blocks Sept 1/Sept 5 deadline
- **Owner**: PM (local-board)
- **Action**: PM unblocks by reallocating resources, contractor support, or scope adjustment
- **Example**: Community calls insufficient → PM hires contractor caller, or reduces target from 100 to 80 ambassadors

**Level 3: PM → Leadership (4-24 hour decision)**
- **Trigger**: Critical blocker (Sept 1 or Sept 5 at risk), affects auto-approval, or requires budget increase
- **Owner**: PM escalates to CEO
- **Action**: CEO makes go/delay/descope decision
- **Example**: Vendor can't deliver payment system by Aug 28 → CEO decides: switch vendor, manual payout process, or delay Sept 1 launch

**Level 4: All-Hands Emergency (Same-hour decision)**
- **Trigger**: Critical production issue Sept 1+, or blocker threatens Sept 6 approval
- **Owner**: PM convenes all-hands call within 4 hours
- **Action**: All teams discuss, decide on hotfix vs. workaround vs. accept risk
- **Example**: Portal performance issues Sept 1 → All-hands call, decide on optimization plan, adjust expectations if needed

### **SPECIFIC DECISION RULES**

| Scenario | Decision Owner | Timeline | Options |
|----------|---|----------|---------|
| **Feature won't ship by Aug 28** | PM + Eng Lead | 2 hours | De-scope (remove from MVP) / Add contractor / Extend scope to Sept 2 |
| **Marketing content 80% vs 95% by Aug 30** | PM + Marketing | 4 hours | Ship as-is (95% is good) / Add freelancer / Reduce post frequency |
| **Only 60 ambassadors vs 80 target by Sept 5** | PM + Community | 4 hours | Accept 60 (still successful) / Extend Sept 1-5 recruitment / Accept conditioned approval |
| **Portal performance issues Sept 1** | PM + Eng Lead | 4 hours | Optimize / Reduce feature set / Accept degradation if not critical / Delay 6-12 hours |
| **Payment vendor not ready by Aug 28** | PM + Finance | 2 hours | Switch vendor / Manual payout process / Delay payouts to Sept 30+ |
| **Critical bug found Sept 5** | PM + Eng Lead | Immediate | Fix if 2-hour fix / Document + hotfix Sept 2 / Accept risk if non-critical |
