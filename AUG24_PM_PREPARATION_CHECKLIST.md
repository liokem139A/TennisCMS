## DECISION FRAMEWORK FOR KICKOFFS

### If Team Lead Says "We need more time"
**Your response**: "What's the minimum viable version we can ship by Sept 1? What do we cut from Phase 1?"

### If Team Lead Says "We don't have enough resources"
**Your response**: "What can we de-scope? What if we hire a contractor? What if we extend a deadline?"

### If Team Lead Says "This timeline is unrealistic"
**Your response**: "I hear you. Let's identify the must-haves for Sept 1. The rest can be Phase 2."

### If Team Lead Says "We're ready to go"
**Your response**: "Awesome. Here's how we measure success by Sept 1..."
