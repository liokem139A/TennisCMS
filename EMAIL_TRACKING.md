## RESPONSE TYPE TRACKING

When logging responses, categorize as:
- **HIGH INTEREST**: "Yes, let's set up a call" or "Send me more info"
- **MEDIUM INTEREST**: "Interesting, let me check with my team" or "Good timing, revisit in Sept"
- **LOW INTEREST**: "Not this year" or "Not a fit"
- **NO RESPONSE**: After 5 business days, mark as non-response (may follow up)

sponsorship partnership opportunity.

In case it got lost in your inbox, I'd love to grab 30 minutes to explore 
how we might work together. Let me know if [three new time options] work better.

Best,
[Your Name]

Thanks,
