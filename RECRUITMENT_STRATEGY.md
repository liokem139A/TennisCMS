# Ambassador Recruitment Strategy & Tactics

**Purpose**: Define recruitment channels, messaging, timeline, and process for recruiting 50-100 ambassadors  
**Timeline**: September 6-30, 2026  
**Target**: 50-100 recruited and onboarded ambassadors  
**Owner**: Marketing Manager + Regional Coordinators

---

## Executive Summary

The recruitment strategy leverages five primary channels to identify and recruit qualified ambassadors:
1. **Club Partnerships** (40% target) - Direct outreach to established club networks
2. **Community Referrals** (20% target) - Ambassador referral program and existing members
3. **Coaching Networks** (15% target) - Coaches, trainers, and tennis professionals
4. **Social Media & Content** (15% target) - Targeted campaigns and organic reach
5. **Events & In-Person** (10% target) - Tennis tournaments and community gatherings

## Channel Strategy

### Channel 1: Club Partnerships (40% of target)

**Rationale**: Club directors and staff know their members best and can refer qualified candidates with high conversion rates.

**Approach**
1. Identify and research top 20-30 target clubs in service area
2. Schedule 30-min partnership meetings with club directors/owners
3. Present ambassador program and mutual benefits
4. Get warm referrals of 5-10 potential ambassadors per club
5. Club director becomes "ambassador champion" and advocate
6. Offer club partnership incentives (group discounts, events, recognition)

**Recruitment Materials**
- Club partnership one-pager (PDF)
- Ambassador program presentation deck (20 slides)
- Sample club member outreach email
- Program overview video (3-5 min)

**Expected Metrics**
- 20-30 clubs engaged
- 5-8 referred candidates per club
- Conversion rate: 70-80%
- Target ambassadors from channel: 35-40

**Timeline**
- Outreach: Sept 6-10
- Meetings: Sept 8-15
- Referrals received: Sept 10-20
- Applications: Sept 15-25

**Contact Strategy**
- Initial outreach: Email + LinkedIn message
- Follow-up: Phone call within 2 days
- Meeting format: 30-min virtual or in-person
- Relationship: Ongoing partnership touchpoint

---

### Channel 2: Community Referrals (20% of target)

**Rationale**: Existing engaged members are most likely to refer similarly engaged friends and colleagues.

**Approach**
1. Launch referral program with incentives
2. Email to all known community members and past participants
3. Social media campaign highlighting referral rewards
4. Ambassador testimonials and success stories
5. "Bring a friend" incentive structure
6. Make it easy for members to refer contacts

**Referral Incentives**
- Refer 1 person: Free merchandise ($25 value)
- Refer 2-3 people: $50 program credit
- Refer 4+ people: $100 credit + special recognition
- If referred person becomes ambassador: $100 bonus + 6 months discount

**Recruitment Materials**
- Referral program announcement (email + social)
- "Tell a Friend" shareable graphics
- Email template for personal referrals
- Ambassador testimonial videos (3-5)
- Referral tracking link/form

**Expected Metrics**
- 1,000+ email reach
- 5-10% engagement rate (50-100 clicks)
- 20-30 referrals submitted
- Conversion rate: 40-60%
- Target ambassadors from channel: 10-15

**Timeline**
- Launch: Sept 6
- Active period: Sept 6-25
- Applications: Sept 12-28

---

### Channel 3: Coaching Networks (15% of target)

**Rationale**: Coaches and tennis professionals have credibility and access to motivated players.

**Approach**
1. Identify and research 30-50 coaches, trainers, and tennis professionals
2. Send personalized outreach with program overview
3. Offer coaching network exclusive benefits
4. Request they share program with clients and peers
5. Position as additional income/community building opportunity
6. Provide coaches with recruitment materials to share

**Coaching Incentives**
- $50 bonus per referred coach who becomes ambassador
- Free premium membership for coach community
- Co-marketing opportunities
- Opportunity to lead clinics or events
- Special recognition in program communications

**Recruitment Materials**
- Coach-specific program overview
- Talking points for coaches to share
- Client email template with coach recommendation
- Bonus program details and structure
- Coach community invitation

**Expected Metrics**
- 30-50 coaches identified and contacted
- 40-50% response rate (15-20 coaches engaged)
- 2-3 referrals per engaged coach (30-60 referrals)
- Conversion rate: 40-50%
- Target ambassadors from channel: 10-15

**Timeline**
- Outreach: Sept 6-10
- Response: Sept 8-15
- Referrals received: Sept 12-25
- Applications: Sept 15-28

### Channel 4: Social Media & Content (15% of target)

**Rationale**: Broad, cost-effective reach to tennis enthusiasts online with compelling messaging.

**Approach**
1. Create recruitment-focused content series (stories, reels, posts)

3. Highlight ambassador benefits and testimonials
4. Create urgency with limited recruitment window messaging

**Content Calendar**

**Paid Advertising** ($1,500-2,000 budget)

- Targeting: Tennis enthusiasts, club members, age 18-65
- Primary conversions: Website clicks to application
- Lookalike audiences based on existing community

**Expected Metrics**
- 10,000-15,000 social media impressions
- 5-10% engagement rate (500-1,500 interactions)
- 3-5% click-through rate (300-750 website visits)
- 10-15% application rate (30-75 applications)
- Conversion rate: 40-60%
- Target ambassadors from channel: 8-12

**Timeline**
- Content creation: Aug 24-Sept 5
- Launch: Sept 6
- Active period: Sept 6-30
- Applications: Ongoing through Sept 25

## Target Candidate Profile

### Ideal Ambassador Characteristics

**Demographic**
- Age: 18-65 (diverse age representation)
- Location: Within 15 miles of target club
- Professional: All backgrounds (students, professionals, retirees)
- Tennis experience: Beginner to advanced (all levels welcome)

**Psychographic**
- Passion for tennis and community
- Reliable and accountable
- Good communicator and interpersonally skilled
- Interested in volunteering or part-time leadership
- Aligned with program values and mission
- Motivated by combination of monetary and non-monetary incentives

**Behavioral**
- Active tennis participant (plays 1+ times per week)
- Engaged in club or community activities
- Has social network within tennis community
- Uses social media (at least passively)
- Available 5-8 hours per month

### Selection Criteria & Rubric

**High Priority Candidates**
- Strong existing network in club
- Previous leadership or volunteer experience
- High engagement level with community
- Clear motivation and enthusiasm
- Available for commitment
- Positive feedback from references

**Strong Candidates**
- Good community presence
- Some relevant experience
- Good communication skills
- Flexible availability
- Positive attitude and reliability

**Consider Candidates**
- Growing interest in community
- Limited but valid experience
- Good potential for growth
- Available and committed
- Trainable skills

**Not a Good Fit**
- Limited community connection
- Reliability concerns
- Communication or interpersonal issues
- Scheduling conflicts
- Misalignment with values

## Application & Selection Process

### Application Form (Online)

```
1. Basic Information
   - Name, email, phone, club
   - Years playing tennis, current level

2. Motivation & Fit
   - Why interested in ambassador role?
   - What experience do you have leading or organizing?
   - How many hours/month can you commit?

3. Community Impact
   - How many people know you in your club?
   - What activity or community group are you most involved in?
   - Describe a time you helped others or led a group

4. Reference
   - Provide 1 club member or coach reference
   - Provide email/phone for reference check

5. Opt-In Questions (by role)
   - [ ] Interested in Club Ambassador role?
   - [ ] Interested in Regional Coordinator role?
   - [ ] Interested in Program Ambassador/Influencer role?
```

### Selection Timeline

**Application Window**: Sept 6-25
- Applications accepted rolling basis
- Emails reviewed within 24-48 hours
- Confirmations sent immediately

**Reference Checks**: Sept 16-28
- Contact references within 2 days of application
- Ask 3-4 specific questions about reliability and leadership
- Document feedback

**Candidate Interviews**: Sept 18-25
- 15-20 min phone or video interviews
- Structured questions to assess fit
- Discuss role expectations and compensation
- Answer candidate questions

**Selection & Offers**: Sept 26
- Compare candidates using scoring rubric
- Select top 50-100 candidates
- Send offer letters and acceptance packages
- Confirm acceptance within 48 hours
