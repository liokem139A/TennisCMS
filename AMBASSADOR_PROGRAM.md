## Program Vision

The Tennis Community Ambassador Program creates a dedicated network of passionate tennis enthusiasts who serve as brand representatives and community leaders across target clubs. Ambassadors drive awareness, recruitment, engagement, and retention while building a vibrant community around tennis.

## Program Objectives

1. **Grow Community**: Recruit 50-100 engaged ambassadors by September 30
2. **Increase Visibility**: Establish presence in target clubs and communities
3. **Drive Engagement**: Create ambassador-led activities and events
4. **Build Loyalty**: Develop champions who advocate for the community long-term
5. **Generate Insights**: Collect feedback and market intelligence from club leaders

## Phase 1: Program Design (Aug 24 - Sept 5)

### Deliverable 1.1: Ambassador Roles & Responsibilities

#### Primary Roles

**Club Ambassador**
- Serves as primary contact between community and target club
- Recruits new members within their club
- Organizes monthly activities (tournaments, socials, clinics)
- Gathers feedback from club members
- Reports attendance and engagement metrics
- Expected time commitment: 5-8 hours/month

**Regional Coordinator**
- Oversees ambassadors in a geographic region (2-3 clubs)
- Facilitates cross-club events and competitions
- Provides support and training to club ambassadors
- Escalates issues and opportunities
- Expected time commitment: 10-12 hours/month

**Program Ambassador (Influencer)**
- High-visibility individuals (coaches, tournament directors, local celebrities)
- Promote program at events and online
- Provide credibility and attract top talent
- Occasional speaking/appearance roles
- Expected time commitment: 3-5 hours/month

#### Core Responsibilities (All Roles)

- Act as community champion and brand representative
- Maintain professional, inclusive communication
- Report monthly engagement metrics
- Participate in quarterly training sessions
- Provide feedback on program improvements
- Attend at least 2 program-organized events per quarter
- Represent the program honestly and authentically

#### Success Metrics per Role

**Club Ambassador**: 
- Recruit 5-10 new members per month
- Host 1-2 organized activities per month
- Achieve 80%+ attendance at program events
- Maintain 4.5+ star feedback rating

**Regional Coordinator**:
- Support 2-3 club ambassadors effectively
- Increase ambassador retention to 90%+
- Organize 1 cross-club event per quarter
- Maintain 4.7+ star feedback rating

**Program Ambassador**:
- Generate 500+ social media impressions per month
- Attend 2+ program events per quarter
- Provide testimonials/content for marketing
- Maintain 4.8+ star feedback rating

### Deliverable 1.2: Incentive Structure

#### Tiered Incentive Program

**Tier 1: Monthly Incentives (All Ambassadors)**
- Digital badge and community recognition
- Early access to new features
- Monthly swag (branded items: hats, socks, water bottles)
- Discounts on program merchandise (15-20%)
- Monthly performance bonus pool ($50-100 per tier level)

**Tier 2: Quarterly Incentives (Top 25% performers)**
- $200-500 quarterly bonus
- Premium branded gear (jackets, bags, apparel)
- Exclusive event invitations
- Opportunity to feature in program marketing/newsletter
- Sponsor connections and partnership opportunities

**Tier 3: Annual Incentives (Top 10% performers)**
- $1,000-2,000 annual bonus
- Luxury gifts (tech items, experiences)
- "Ambassador of the Year" award and public recognition
- Free access to premium coaching/clinics
- Speaking opportunity at annual summit
- Direct line to product/community team

#### Non-Monetary Incentives

- **Recognition**: Monthly leaderboards, community spotlights, annual awards
- **Community**: Exclusive ambassador group chats, networking events
- **Development**: Free training, leadership workshops, certification programs
- **Influence**: Voice in program decisions, feedback committees
- **Access**: Early feature access, exclusive content, event passes

#### Bonus Structure Details

**Monthly Performance Bonuses** (Distributed within tier):
- Club Ambassador: $50/month for 90%+ performance
- Regional Coordinator: $75/month for 90%+ performance
- Program Ambassador: $100/month for content/engagement targets

**Performance Tracking**
- Recruitment numbers
- Event attendance and hosting
- Community feedback ratings
- Retention metrics
- Content contribution quality

#### Total Program Cost Estimate

- Swag & merchandise: $15-25/ambassador/month
- Monthly bonuses: $50-100/ambassador/month
- Quarterly incentives: $200-500/top performer/quarter
- Annual awards: $1,000-2,000/top performers

**Annual budget for 75 ambassadors: ~$45,000-60,000**

### Deliverable 1.3: Onboarding Materials

#### Ambassador Onboarding Package (Digital + Physical)

**Welcome Materials**
- Welcome video from CEO/Community Lead (2-3 min)
- Program overview brochure (digital + printed)
- Role-specific playbooks (10-15 pages each)
- Code of conduct and community guidelines
- FAQ document (common questions + answers)

**Training Program** (Online + In-Person)

*Required: All Ambassadors (3 hours)*
- Module 1: Community vision and values (15 min video + quiz)
- Module 2: Communication guidelines and brand voice (20 min video + workshop)
- Module 3: Activity planning and event facilitation (30 min video + templates)
- Module 4: Using the ambassador portal (20 min demo)
- Module 5: Reporting metrics and feedback (15 min guide)
- Live Q&A and introduction (60 min virtual session)

*Role-Specific Training (2 hours each)*
- Club Ambassador: Recruitment strategies, member retention, local event planning
- Regional Coordinator: Team management, cross-club coordination, escalation processes
- Program Ambassador: Content creation, public speaking, media engagement

**Ongoing Support**
- Monthly community calls (1st Friday, 60 min)
- Private ambassador Slack/Discord channel
- Dedicated support email (ambassador-support@community.com)
- Quarterly in-person regional meetups
- 1-on-1 onboarding calls with program coordinator (30 min)

#### Physical Onboarding Kit

- Welcome letter and program guide
- Branded ambassador merch pack (cap, shirt, water bottle)
- Local activity planning toolkit
- Business cards with ambassador designation
- QR code linking to digital resources

### Deliverable 1.4: Ambassador Portal & Dashboard

#### Portal Features & Specifications

**User Authentication**
- Email/password login with 2-factor option
- Google/Apple sign-in integration
- Role-based access control (Club, Regional, Influencer)
- Mobile-responsive design

**Dashboard - "My Ambassador Hub"**

*Performance View*
- Monthly recruitment numbers vs. target
- Event attendance and hosting metrics
- Member feedback/ratings (aggregated)
- Bonus earnings and incentive tracking
- Certification status and training completion
- Leaderboard ranking (opt-in visibility)

*Calendar & Events*
- Personal event calendar
- Sync with community calendar
- Event creation and management tools
- RSVP tracking for hosted events
- Reminder notifications

*Resources & Tools*
- Template library (emails, social posts, event flyers)
- Brand guidelines and asset downloads
- Activity ideas and best practices
- Training materials and videos
- FAQ and help documentation

*Communication Hub*
- Direct messaging with program coordinator
- Group chat for regional ambassadors
- Notification preferences and settings
- Announcement feed from program leadership

**Admin Features** (Program Team Only)
- Ambassador management (add, edit, suspend)
- Performance monitoring and analytics
- Payment/bonus management
- Content moderation
- Reporting and export tools
- Program analytics dashboard

#### Technical Specifications

**Technology Stack**
- Frontend: React or Vue.js for responsive UI
- Backend: Node.js/Express or Python Flask
- Database: PostgreSQL for relational data
- Authentication: OAuth 2.0 + JWT tokens
- Hosting: Cloud provider (AWS/GCP/Azure) with auto-scaling
- Mobile: React Native or Progressive Web App (PWA)

**Data & Analytics**
- Track recruitment metrics
- Monitor event participation
- Analyze feedback sentiment
- Identify top performers
- Generate program-wide engagement reports
- Export data for analysis

**Security & Compliance**
- SSL/TLS encryption for all data in transit
- Password hashing (bcrypt or similar)
- Regular security audits
- GDPR-compliant data handling
- Rate limiting and DDoS protection
- Backup and disaster recovery plan

**Integrations**
- Google Calendar API for event sync
- Email system for notifications
- Slack/Discord for ambassador community
- Analytics tools (Mixpanel or Segment)
- CRM system for member data

## Phase 2: Recruitment (Sept 6 - Sept 30)

### Target Profile

**Ideal Ambassador**
- Tennis enthusiast or club member (beginner to advanced player)
- Active in local tennis community
- Good communication and leadership skills
- Reliable and organized
- Passionate about growing the sport
- 3+ hours/week available commitment
- Located in target club service area

**Priority Recruitment Channels**
1. Club partnerships (outreach to club directors, coaches)
2. Existing community members (referral program)
3. University/college tennis programs
4. Community social media and online groups
5. Local tennis events and tournaments
6. Coaching networks and tennis professionals

### Recruitment Timeline

- **Sept 6-10**: Begin outreach to club contacts and partners
- **Sept 11-20**: Launch social media and referral campaigns
- **Sept 21-25**: Conduct interviews and finalize selections
