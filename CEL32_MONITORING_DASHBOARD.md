## ENGAGEMENT SCORING REFERENCE

**Quick Reference for Tier Assignment**:

| Action | Points | Tier Impact |
|--------|--------|------------|
| Email delivered | +5 | Baseline |
| Email opened | +10 | Moves to Tier 3+ |
| Email opened 3x+ | +15 | Moves to Tier 2+ |
| Link clicked | +25 | Moves to Tier 2+ |
| Reply received | +50 | Moves to Tier 1+ |
| Calendar accepted | +50 | Moves to Tier 1 |
| Meeting request | +100 | Confirms Tier 1 |

**Tier Ranges**:
- **Tier 1 (Hot)**: 75+ points (meeting committed or strong positive reply)
- **Tier 2 (Warm)**: 35-74 points (multiple opens + click, or positive reply)
- **Tier 3 (Cool)**: 5-34 points (single open, minimal engagement)
- **Tier 4 (Cold)**: <5 points (no engagement)
