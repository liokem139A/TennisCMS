## Phone Outreach Script (if calling first)

**Opening** (30 seconds):
```
"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from [Company]. I'm calling because 
I think there might be an interesting opportunity for us to explore together 
around community partnerships. Do you have 2 minutes right now?"

[IF YES, continue; IF NO, ask: "What's a better time this week?"]
```

**Pitch** (60-90 seconds):
```
"We're building [Community Name] — a platform that brings together 
[tennis players/fitness enthusiasts/community members]. What we're 
realizing is that the [community] is looking for brands they can 
trust and engage with authentically.

That's where [Company Name] comes in. You've always been a leader in 
[this space], and we think there's a real opportunity to partner.

Rather than jumping into a detailed proposal right now, I'd love to 
have a 30-45 minute conversation where I learn about your goals and 
we explore whether this makes sense for both of us.

Does that sound interesting?"

[IF YES: "Great! Let me send you a calendar link to pick a time that works..."]
[IF NO: "Totally understand. If anything changes, feel free to reach out."]
```

## Key Tips for Outreach Success

**DO**:
- ✅ Personalize every email - mention specific facts about their company
- ✅ Lead with genuine interest in THEIR goals, not just sponsorship money
- ✅ Keep initial ask small (30-45 min conversation, not a full pitch)
- ✅ Show you understand their business and what they care about
- ✅ Be clear about next steps and make it easy to say yes
- ✅ Follow up, but don't be pushy (2-3 touch points max)

**DON'T**:
- ❌ Generic email blast (shows lack of effort)
- ❌ Lead with sponsorship packages/pricing (too salesy)
- ❌ Make big asks upfront (conversation first, not proposal)
- ❌ Copy/paste templates verbatim (personalize!)
- ❌ Overwhelm with information (keep it conversational)
