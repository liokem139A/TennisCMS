## EMAIL STRATEGY

**Overall Approach**:
- Personalized (research the prospect before sending)
- Specific value proposition (not generic)
- Clear call-to-action (30-min discovery call)
- Brief and respectful of time
- Authentic community/sport focus

**Best Practices**:
1. Research prospect's recent news/initiatives first
2. Customize subject line and first line
3. Explain WHY you're reaching out to them specifically
4. Make the ask simple (30 min call)
5. Provide 2-3 specific time options
6. Include direct phone number for callbacks
