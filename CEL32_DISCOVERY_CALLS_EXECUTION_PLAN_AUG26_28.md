## DISCOVERY CALL FRAMEWORK

### Purpose of Each Call
**Duration**: 45 minutes per call  
**Objective**: Understand club's goals, challenges, and partnership fit  
**Success Metric**: 80%+ move to next phase (proposal discussion)

### Call Structure (45 minutes)

**Introduction** (5 min)
- [ ] Thank them for the time and interest
- [ ] Briefly introduce self and Celadon Tennis mission
- [ ] Set agenda for the call
- Example: "Thanks for making time for this, Russ. In the next 45 minutes, I want to understand your club's goals, learn about any challenges you're facing, and explore whether our platform could be valuable for your members. Does that sound good?"

**Understanding Their World** (12 min)
- [ ] What's most important to your club right now?
- [ ] Who are your members? (demographics, skill levels, interests)
- [ ] What are the biggest pain points in how you run your club today?
- [ ] How do you currently handle [tournaments / member engagement / scheduling]?
- [ ] What's working well that you want to preserve?

**Value Proposition** (12 min)
- [ ] Share Celadon Tennis platform overview (2 min)
  - AI-powered community tools
  - Digital tournament management
  - Member engagement platform
  - Real-time analytics & reporting
- [ ] Address their specific challenges (5 min)
  - "Based on what you shared, here's how we specifically help with [challenge]..."
  - Concrete examples from similar clubs
- [ ] Show demo or case study (3 min, if time allows)
  - Key features relevant to their needs
  - Implementation timeline
  - Support structure

**Next Steps & Decision** (12 min)
- [ ] What questions do you have about our platform?
- [ ] Do you think this could be valuable for your club?
- [ ] What would it take for you to move forward?
- [ ] Timeline: When would you need something like this implemented?
- [ ] Budget/Approval: Who else needs to be involved in the decision?

**Close & Follow-up** (4 min)
- [ ] Confirm next steps (proposal, follow-up call, demo, etc.)
- [ ] Set timeline for proposal (if moving forward)
- [ ] Offer specific support during evaluation
- [ ] Thank them again
