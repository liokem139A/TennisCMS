# Calendly Setup — 30 Minute Quick Start
**Status**: Required by Aug 26 @ 9 AM (or use explicit time slots in emails)  
**Time to Complete**: 30-45 minutes  
**Difficulty**: Easy  

---

## QUICK SUMMARY

Calendly lets prospects book 30-min discovery calls directly without back-and-forth emails. If this setup takes too long, **the emails already have explicit time slots as a backup** — emails are ready to send either way.

---

## STEP 1: CREATE ACCOUNT (5 min)

1. Go to **calendly.com**
2. Click **Sign Up**
3. Use your work email
4. Create password
5. Skip the tour (click "Skip" at bottom)

---

## STEP 2: CONNECT YOUR CALENDAR (5 min)

1. Click **⚙️ Settings** (top right)
2. Click **Calendar Connection**
3. Select your calendar type (Gmail/Outlook)
4. Click **Connect**
5. Authorize Calendly (follow prompts)

---

## STEP 3: CREATE DISCOVERY CALL EVENT (15 min)

1. Click **Event Types** (left menu)
2. Click **Create Event Type**
3. Fill in:
   - **Event Name**: `Sponsor Discovery Call`
   - **Duration**: `30 minutes`
   - **Availability**: Calendar your connected calendar
   - **Organizer**: Your email

4. Click **Next: Configure**

5. Under "Event Details":
   - **Location**: `Zoom Meeting` (or Phone Call)
   - **Description**: (leave blank or add: "30-minute sponsor discovery call")

6. Under "Invitee Questions":
   - **Add question**: "Company Name" (required)
   - **Add question**: "What's your main sponsorship priority?" (optional)

7. Click **Next: Configure Availability**

8. Under "Hours":
   - **Monday-Friday**: Toggle **ON**
   - **Time slots**:
     ```
     10:00 AM - 12:00 PM (3 slots available)
     2:00 PM - 4:30 PM (5 slots available)
     ```
   - **Saturday-Sunday**: Toggle **OFF**

9. **Add exception for Aug 26-27 ONLY** (for evening slots with busy execs):
   - Click **+ Add Date/Time Exception**
   - Dates: Aug 26 & Aug 27
   - Add: 6:00 PM - 8:00 PM
   - Save

10. Click **Buffer** (add buffer time):
    - Before event: `15 minutes`
    - After event: `15 minutes`

11. Click **Save** (top right)

---

## STEP 4: ADD ZOOM (5 min)

1. Click **⚙️ Settings** → **Integrations**
2. Search for **Zoom**
3. Click **Connect**
4. Click **Authorize** (follow Zoom prompts)
5. Return to Calendly → Integrations should show **Zoom: Connected** ✓

---

## STEP 5: GET YOUR LINK (2 min)

1. Click **Event Types** → **Sponsor Discovery Call**
2. Scroll down to **Sharing**
3. Copy the link that looks like:
   ```
   calendly.com/[your-name]/sponsor-discovery-30min
   ```
4. **Save this link** — you'll add it to emails

---

## STEP 6: TEST IT (5 min)

1. Open your Calendly link in a new browser tab
2. Try booking a test appointment:
   - Pick a time (not a real time you'll be busy)
   - Fill in fake details
   - Click **Confirm**
3. Check:
   - [ ] Confirmation email arrives
   - [ ] Email includes Zoom link
   - [ ] Zoom link works
4. **Delete the test appointment** (click Calendar → delete)

---

## STEP 7: UPDATE EMAILS (2 min)

1. Open **OUTREACH_EMAILS_FINAL.md**
2. Find every `[CALENDLY LINK]` placeholder
3. Replace with your actual link:
   ```
   calendly.com/[your-name]/sponsor-discovery-30min
   ```
4. Save the file

---

## ✅ DONE

Your Calendly is live. Prospects can book directly on Aug 26+ when they receive emails.

---

## ⚠️ WHAT IF CALENDLY SETUP FAILS?

**No problem** — the emails already have 3 explicit time slots:

**Example from email**:
```
Available times:
• Tuesday, Aug 26 @ 10:00 AM EST
• Tuesday, Aug 26 @ 2:00 PM EST
• Wednesday, Aug 27 @ 6:00 PM EST

Book here: [CALENDLY LINK]
(or reply with your preferred time above)
```

**What to do**:
1. Send emails AS-IS on Aug 26 (explicit times already there)
2. Prospects reply with preferred time
3. You reply with calendar invite
4. Same outcome, just manual instead of automated

---

## COMMON ISSUES

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| **Zoom not showing in Calendly** | Try reconnecting Zoom integration (Settings → Integrations) |
| **Calendar not available** | Make sure you're using the right email account that's connected to your calendar |
| **Time slots showing as booked** | You already have something on your calendar at that time; adjust availability |
| **Link not working** | Try the link in incognito/private browser mode |
| **Confirmation email doesn't have Zoom** | Re-check Zoom integration is connected in Event Settings |
