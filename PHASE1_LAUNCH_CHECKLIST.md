## Risk Mitigation & Contingencies

**Risk**: Low email response rate
- **Mitigation**: Warm intro requests, LinkedIn connection before email, phone follow-up
- **Contingency**: Expand prospect list to 15-20 companies, try shorter discovery format (20-min phone)

**Risk**: Prospects don't schedule calls
- **Mitigation**: Phone outreach after email, multiple contact attempts, offer flexible timing
- **Contingency**: One-on-one LinkedIn messages, offer to meet in person (local prospects)

**Risk**: Low interest signals during discovery
- **Mitigation**: Refine value proposition, expand to different verticals, focus on top 5 strongest prospects
- **Contingency**: Shift focus to strongest fit prospects, modify messaging based on feedback

**Risk**: Budget misalignment
- **Mitigation**: Present flexible package options, validate budget assumptions early in call
- **Contingency**: Adjust package tiers to match market reality, add premium tier if demand signals indicate

**Risk**: Call cancellations/no-shows
- **Mitigation**: Send reminder 24 hours before, confirm attendees, follow up immediately with thank-you
- **Contingency**: Reschedule immediately, maintain flexible calendar, build buffer time
