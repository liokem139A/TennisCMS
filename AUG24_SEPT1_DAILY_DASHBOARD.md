## CRITICAL BLOCKERS & ESCALATION

### Escalation Triggers (Alert PM Immediately)

**AMBER ALERT** (Today/Same Day):
- Any team 1+ day behind schedule
- Critical bugs discovered in code/design
- Vendor contract negotiation stalled
- Resource unavailable unexpectedly
- External dependency blocked (third-party API, etc.)

**RED ALERT** (Emergency, Within 2 hours):
- Team believes they cannot deliver by Sept 1
- Critical architectural issue requiring rework
- Budget overrun >10% ($1,460+)
- Key team member departure

**Escalation Process**:
1. Team lead notifies PM immediately (Slack call or message)
2. PM assesses impact and recovery options
3. If recoverable: PM updates contingency plan, facilitates resource reallocation
4. If not recoverable: PM escalates to leadership for immediate re-prioritization
